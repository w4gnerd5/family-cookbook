# Georgian Khachapuri Recipe (Gia Agirba)

Khachapuri is the quintessential Georgian comfort food, a cheese-filled bread that relies on the harmony between a soft, airy dough and a rich, tangy cheese filling. The key to success lies in using high-quality Imeretian and Sulguni cheeses and ensuring the dough is kneaded "energetically" to develop the gluten necessary to hold the heavy cheese filling without tearing.

## Ingredients
> - 300 ml Water
> - 2 tsp (5g) Yeast
> - Salt
> - Sugar (slightly more than a pinch)
> - A little Milk
> - 450g Flour
> - 30–40 ml Oil
> - 400g Imeretian Cheese
> - 200g Fresh Sulguni Cheese
> - 2 Eggs (to be mixed into the cheese filling)

## Instructions
> 1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the water.
> 2. Add a little milk.
> 3. Combine with the flour (approx. 450g) and knead the dough energetically by hand.
> 4. Once the dough is kneaded, incorporate 30–40 ml of oil.
> 5. Place the dough in a warm spot and cover it thickly to rise.
> 6. Prepare the filling: One khachapuri requires 300g of cheese. Mix 400g Imeretian cheese and 200g fresh Sulguni (total 600g for 2 khachapuri) with 2 eggs.
> 7. When the dough has risen, sprinkle with flour. 
> 8. Portion the dough, then smooth it out gently and thinly without over-handling it.
> 9. Let the prepared dough rest for 10 minutes before baking.

## Notes
For the best texture, ensure the cheese filling is at room temperature before stuffing the dough. The energetic kneading mentioned is vital for creating a dough strong enough to support the traditional 1:1 cheese-to-dough ratio.
