# Scalded Crepes (Zavarnyye Blini)

This traditional Russian recipe uses the "zavarnoye" (scalded) technique, where boiling water is whisked into the cold batter. This process partially gelatinizes the flour's starch, resulting in incredibly thin, elastic, and "lacy" crepes that are easier to flip and have a delicate texture.

## Ingredients

* 1/2 liter milk
* 3 eggs
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 2 tablespoons sugar
* 2 cups flour
* 1/4 cup olive oil or vegetable oil
* 1/2 liter boiling water

## Instructions

1. Mix together the milk, eggs, salt, and sugar. Add the flour and olive/vegetable oil, then beat everything together thoroughly. Gradually add the boiling water while stirring the mixture continuously. And when you have mixed all of this, add 1/2 tsp of baking soda splashed with vinegar. Fry on a frying pan without oil. It is tastier with meat.

## Notes

* It is crucial to whisk the batter vigorously while adding the boiling water in a slow, steady stream to prevent the eggs from curdling. For the best results, let the batter rest for about 10–15 minutes before cooking to allow the flour to fully hydrate.
* To "slake" the soda, place the powder in a small spoon and add just enough vinegar to make it fizz vigorously before stirring it into the mix; this prevents a metallic aftertaste. Use a well-seasoned cast iron or a non-stick pan to ensure the dough releases easily without the use of fat.

* **Other recording:** a second sticky note of this recipe drifts slightly — 2 eggs instead of 3, 2.5 cups flour, 1 teaspoon of soda. This fuller version is canonical.
