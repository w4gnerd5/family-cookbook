# Khachapuri

Khachapuri is a staple of Georgian cuisine, representing a diverse family of cheese-filled breads. This specific dough recipe utilizes a pastry-style technique where cold butter is "chopped" into flour and combined with the acidic lift of matsoni (a traditional fermented milk) and baking soda to create a tender, slightly flaky crust.

## Ingredients

* 1. Flour, 6 tea cups (approx. 130g-150g per cup)
* 2. Butter, 200g (cold)
* 3. Matsoni (or sour yogurt), 800g
* 4. Baking soda, 1 tsp

## Instructions

1. Pour flour onto a cutting board. Coat the butter in the flour and cut it into cubes with a knife.
2. Continue by crumbling the mixture with your hands until it reaches a homogeneous consistency (small grains of butter should remain visible within the flour).
3. Make a well in this flour mixture and pour in the matsoni (or sour yogurt) and 1 teaspoon of baking soda.
4. Prepare the dough by using a large knife to "chop" the ingredients together. Once a homogeneous mass begins to form, you may knead it by hand.
5. When the dough becomes uniform and smooth, divide it into 3 equal parts (enough for 3 baking trays).
6. Place the dough in a closed container and let it rest/rise for 1 hour.

## Notes

* Success depends on keeping the butter cold and using a "chopping" motion with a knife to combine it with the flour, which prevents the fat from melting and ensures a light texture. During kneading, do not add any additional flour; use only the amount specified in the proportions. Before baking, brush the khachapuri with melange (egg wash). Bake in a well-preheated oven.
