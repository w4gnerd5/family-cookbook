# Oven-Baked Brioches

This recipe utilizes a traditional "starter" (preferment) method, which is the secret to achieving a light, airy crumb in yeast-based breads. By first activating the yeast with a small amount of flour and warm water, you ensure a strong ferment that gives these brioches their characteristic soft texture and rich flavor even without excessive dairy.

## Ingredients

> - 3 tablespoons flour
> - 1 teaspoon sugar
> - 1 teaspoon salt
> - 7-8 grams yeast
> - 150 milliliters warm water
> - Separately: 300 grams flour
> - 2 tablespoons oil

## Instructions

> - Mix the first five ingredients (3 tablespoons of flour, sugar, salt, yeast, and warm water) to create a starter.
> - Add the 300 grams of flour and 2 tablespoons of oil to the starter mixture.
> - Knead the dough well until smooth.
> - Let the dough rest and rise for 40 minutes in a warm place.

## Notes

- **Water Temperature:** Ensure the water is lukewarm (around 100°F / 38°C); water that is too hot will kill the yeast, while cold water will significantly slow down the rise.
- **Dough Consistency:** If the dough feels too sticky after kneading, add flour one tablespoon at a time until it no longer sticks to your hands but remains soft.
