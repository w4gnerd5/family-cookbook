# Soft Ginger Cookies

These cookies achieve their signature soft and chewy texture through the combination of molasses and a specific ratio of leavening agents. The molasses provides moisture and acidity that reacts with the baking soda to create a tender crumb, while rolling the dough in sugar before baking adds a contrasting crystalline crunch to every bite.

## Ingredients

* 2 1/4 cups flour
* 2 teaspoons ginger
* 1 teaspoon baking powder OR 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
* 3/4 teaspoon cinnamon
* 1/2 teaspoon cloves
* 1/4 teaspoon salt
* 3/4 cup margarine
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 egg
* 1/4 cup molasses

## Instructions

1. Combine the first 6 ingredients.
2. Add this dry mixture to the creamed mixture (margarine, sugar, and egg) and molasses.
3. Roll dough into balls, then roll them in sugar.
4. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.

## Notes

* Ensure your margarine and egg are at room temperature for a smooth, well-emulsified "creamed" base.
* For the softest results, remove the cookies from the oven when they still look slightly underbaked in the center; they will firm up as they cool.
