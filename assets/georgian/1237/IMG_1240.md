# Puff Pastry Dough Pie - page 4

This traditional recipe emphasizes a specific baking technique where starting with a cold oven is essential for puff pastry to achieve its maximum lift and flakiness. It also incorporates a folk tradition of hiding a lucky charm inside the savory cheese and egg filling to bring fortune for the coming year.

## Ingredients

* Puff pastry dough
* Cheese
* Hard-boiled eggs
* Melted butter
* A button or a coin (for the tradition)

## Instructions

1. * At the beginning, the oven must not be hot; otherwise, the puff pastry will not rise!!!
2. * In the baked pie, make three holes and pour melted butter into them.
3. * (Diagram shows three holes to be made in the top of the pastry)
4. * Read carefully! From this dough, you can make a Christmas "Ghvezeli" filled with cheese and boiled eggs (cut the eggs in half).
5. * Do not forget to put a button or a coin inside and make a wish! Give the pastry the same shape
6. * only without holes and butter
7. * You can substitute mushroom and terragon for cabbage

## Notes

* Success with puff pastry requires starting in a cool oven so the layers can separate and expand before the fat melts and the structure sets. Pouring melted butter into the holes of the finished pie ensures a rich, moist interior that complements the crispy crust.
