# Russian Layered Savory Pie (Pirog) - 3rd page

This recipe describes a traditional Russian "Pirog," a multi-layered savory pastry that combines hearty staples like cabbage, rice, and eggs. The success of this dish relies on the structural layering of cooked ingredients encased in a thin, crisp crust, creating a moist interior that is perfect for a communal family meal.

## Ingredients

> *   Chilled dough 
> *   Cooked rice
> *   Braised cabbage with onions
> *   Hard-boiled eggs
> *   Boiled, finely chopped beef
> *   Black pepper
> *   Water or egg white (for sealing)
> *   Egg yolk and a little water (for egg wash)
> *   Poppy seeds (optional)

## Instructions

> 1. Divide the dough into two parts.
> 2. Roll out the chilled dough to a thickness of 3 mm and transfer it to a baking sheet.
> 3. Layer the filling: first the rice, then the braised cabbage with onion. 
> 4. Top with hard-boiled eggs cut into halves or quarters lengthwise.
> 5. Add boiled and finely chopped beef on top, and season with black pepper.
> 6. Wet the edges of the dough with water or egg white. 
> 7. Roll out the second half of the dough and cover the top. 
> 8. Press the edges with your fingers to seal them together and trim any excess dough. 
> 9. Use the back of a fork to create a decorative pattern along the edge.
> 10. Brush the pie with a mixture of egg yolk and a little water. 
> 11. Optionally, sprinkle with poppy seeds. Prick the top in several places with a fork.
> 12. Bake at 220°C (430°F) for 35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown.

## Notes

The high baking temperature of 220°C requires close monitoring; ensure the top is well-browned but not burnt. Pricking the dough with a fork is essential to allow steam to escape, preventing the crust from becoming soggy or bursting.
