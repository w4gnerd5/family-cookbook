# Puff Pastry

This recipe describes a "rough puff" or flaky pastry dough where the key to success is maintaining the cold temperature of the ingredients. By cutting the butter into the flour rather than fully incorporating it, you create distinct layers of fat that release steam during baking, creating the airy, shattered texture essential for traditional Slavic sweet or savory pies.

## Ingredients
> *   Flour: 250g
> *   Cold butter: 400–450g
> *   Cold water: 6–8 tablespoons
> *   Salt: 1/4 teaspoon

## Instructions
> 1. In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt.
> 2. Cut the chilled butter into cubes (do this directly in the bowl with the flour).
> 3. Continue cutting the butter until the cubes become smaller, approximately 0.5 cm in size.
> 4. Add a little cold water and stir with a fork; if the dough is still crumbly, add a little more water.
> 5. Next, work the dough with your hands and form it into a ball (you shouldn't knead it much by hand so that the butter doesn't melt completely).

## Notes
The most critical factor is temperature. Use ice-cold water and butter straight from the fridge. Work as quickly as possible to ensure the butter remains in distinct chunks within the dough; if the butter melts into the flour before baking, the pastry will be greasy rather than flaky.
