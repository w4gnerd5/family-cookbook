# Kefir Pancakes (Blini) with Baking Soda

These Eastern European pancakes utilize the chemical reaction between acidic kefir (or Matsoni, a traditional Georgian yogurt) and baking soda to create a light, airy texture. Thinning the batter with milk ensures a delicate, pourable consistency typical of traditional thin blini, which are meant to be light enough to fold or roll.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup flour
* 1 cup Matsoni or Kefir
* 1/3 teaspoon baking soda
* Salt
* Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Sift the flour into a bowl, then add the kefir and salt. The batter will be runny at first.
2. Add the baking soda and mix well with a spoon.
3. Gradually add more flour and mix until combined.
4. Add the vegetable oil and stir in.
5. Let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
6. Grease the pan with oil and cook the blini over medium heat.

## Notes

* **Consistency:** The key to this recipe is the "liquid dough" (жидкое тесто); the batter should be thin enough to spread across the pan quickly when poured.
* **Leavening:** For the best rise, ensure your kefir or matsoni is at room temperature so it reacts effectively with the baking soda.
