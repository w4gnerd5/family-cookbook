# Achma-Makarina

This traditional Georgian recipe utilizes a high egg count and a "feel-based" flour measurement to create a rich, elastic dough. The technique of adding flour "until it's kneaded in" ensures a tender crust that can support a heavy, savory cheese filling without becoming tough or dry, a hallmark of homemade regional breads like Khachapuri.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup water
* 5 eggs
* Salt (to taste)
* Flour (as much as the mixture absorbs)
* Cheese: 600g
* Butter: 200g

## Instructions

1. Combine 1 cup of water with 5 eggs. Add salt and gradually incorporate flour, kneading until the dough reaches the proper consistency ("as much as it absorbs").

## Notes

* When adding the flour, aim for a soft, slightly tacky, but elastic texture; avoid adding too much flour, as the dough should remain supple. Ensure the cheese is well-crumbled and mixed with the butter for an even, melty filling.
