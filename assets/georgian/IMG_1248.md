# Satsivi for One Large Turkey

Satsivi is the crown jewel of Georgian festive cuisine, traditionally served cold during Christmas and New Year celebrations. The dish's unique chemistry relies on the high natural oil content of finely ground walnuts and a specific aromatic blend of warming spices—like blue fenugreek and marigold—which emulsify with the broth to create a rich, velvety sauce without the need for additional thickeners.

## Ingredients

> * 1 Large Turkey
> * Walnuts: 600g (finely ground)
> * Dried Coriander: 1 dessert spoon
> * Blue Fenugreek (Utskho Suneli): 1/2 dessert spoon
> * Savory: A small amount
> * Marigold (Yellow Flower): 1/2 dessert spoon
> * Red Pepper: To taste
> * Black Pepper: On the tip of a knife
> * Cloves: On the tip of a knife
> * Cinnamon: On the tip of a knife
> * White Vinegar: 1 tablespoon
> * Eggs: 2 pieces
> * Salt: To taste (for everything)
> * Garlic: 4-5 cloves (processed in a food processor)

## Instructions

> 1. Boil the turkey separately.
> 2. Sauté a substantial amount of finely chopped onions in the fat rendered from the turkey broth. 
> 3. Do not let the onions brown or burn; they should remain pale and soft.
> 4. (Note: It is easier if the onions are processed in a food processor before sautéing).

## Notes

* **Texture:** For the most authentic result, grind the walnuts until they are as fine as flour to ensure a completely smooth sauce.
* **Onion Prep:** When sautéing the onions in the broth fat, keep the heat low to ensure they soften completely without caramelizing, which would alter the classic pale color of the sauce.
* **Serving:** Satsivi must be prepared in advance and served cold or at room temperature to allow the walnut sauce to thicken naturally and the spices to fully bloom.
