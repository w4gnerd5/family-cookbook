# Vegetable Enchilada Casserole

This dish is a hearty vegetarian take on traditional enchiladas, layering classic Tex-Mex flavors into a convenient casserole format. The combination of beans, corn, and zucchini provides a satisfying texture, while layering the tortillas instead of rolling them ensures every bite is thoroughly infused with sauce and melted cheese.

## Ingredients

* Oil
* Onions
* Zucchini
* Beans
* Tomatoes
* Corn
* Cumin & Salt
* Enchilada sauce
* Tortillas
* Cheese

## Instructions

1. Sauté onions in oil, add zucchini, beans, tomatoes, corn, cumin & salt.
2. Cook til hot.
3. In a 9×13 pan, layer in this order:
4. 1. Enchilada sauce
5. 2. Corn tortillas
6. 3. 1/2 vegetables
7. 4. Cheese
8. 5. Repeat, end cheese
9. Cover with foil.
10. Bake at 400°.
11. Bake 15 min covered, then 15 min uncovered.

## Notes

* To ensure the casserole sets properly, make sure the vegetable mixture is steaming hot before layering.
* Removing the foil for the final half of the baking time is essential for achieving a bubbly, golden-brown cheese topping.
