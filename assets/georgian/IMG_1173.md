# Technique for Shaping Georgian Shoti Bread

This instructional note outlines the specific manual shaping technique for traditional Georgian bread, likely *Shoti*. The process of thinning the dough in the center while maintaining thick edges is crucial for the bread to properly adhere to the curved walls of a *tone* (clay oven) and to achieve the signature elongated shape with a soft middle and crisp, raised crust.

## Ingredients
> *   No specific ingredients listed (The original text focuses exclusively on dough preparation and shaping steps).

## Instructions
> 1. Knead the dough well.
> 2. Flatten the dough by hand in the middle, ensuring you leave the edges thick.
> 3. Do not allow the sides to become thin.
> 4. Stretch and flatten the dough out lengthwise.
> 5. Let it rest for 2 hours.
> 6. Let it sit, then rotate and stretch it in your hands to finalize the shape.

## Notes
> Maintaining the thickness of the rim is essential; it prevents the bread from tearing when stretched and helps it cling to the oven walls. The two-hour resting period is vital for gluten relaxation, which allows for the final hand-rotation without the dough springing back.
