# "Eskimo" Cake

"Eskimo" cake is a beloved nostalgic dessert in Georgian cuisine, named for its resemblance to the creamy texture and chocolate coating of a classic ice cream bar. The recipe relies on a dual-part cream—a cooked custard folded into whipped buttercream—and a light sponge leavened by the chemical reaction of vinegar and baking soda combined with aerated egg whites.

## Ingredients

> **For the Sponge:**
> * 300g Sugar
> * 4 Egg yolks (whisked with sugar into "gogli-mogli")
> * 7 tbsp Milk
> * 3 tbsp Sour cream (Smetana)
> * 1.5 cups Flour
> * 1 tsp Baking soda
> * 1 tbsp Walnuts
> * 4 Egg whites (beaten separately)
>
> **For the Custard (Part I):**
> * 4 Egg yolks
> * 1.5 cups Sugar
> * 3 tbsp Flour
> * 0.5 L Milk
>
> **For the Buttercream (Part II):**
> * 250g Butter
> * 200g Sugar
> * Vanilla
>
> **For the Glaze:**
> * 1 cup Sugar
> * 2 tbsp Cocoa powder
> * 6 tbsp Milk
> * 100g Butter

## Instructions

> **Preparing the Sponge:**
> Whisk the 4 egg yolks with 300g of sugar until pale. Add the milk, sour cream, flour, baking soda, and walnuts. In a separate bowl, whisk the 4 egg whites, then gently fold them into the dough. Bake on buttered parchment paper at a low heat.
>
> **Preparing the Cream:**
> 1. To make the custard (Part I), combine 4 egg yolks, 1.5 cups of sugar, 3 tablespoons of flour, and 0.5 liters of milk. Cook the mixture over heat (boil) until thickened.
> 2. To make the buttercream (Part II), whisk 250g of butter with 200g of sugar and vanilla until fluffy.
> 3. Once the custard (Part I) has cooled completely, whisk it into the buttercream (Part II).
>
> **Preparing the Glaze:**
> Combine 1 cup of sugar, 2 tablespoons of cocoa, 6 tablespoons of milk, and 100g of butter. Bring the mixture to a boil while stirring constantly. Once the glaze has cooled slightly, brush it over the finished cake.

## Notes

Ensure the custard base is completely cooled to room temperature before mixing it with the whipped butter to prevent the cream from curdling or melting. For the sponge, bake at a low temperature (approximately 325°F/160°C) to maintain a soft, light texture without drying it out.
