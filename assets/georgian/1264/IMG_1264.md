# Ajika

Ajika is a bold and fragrant Georgian condiment, traditionally defined by its fiery pepper base and pungent garlic. This specific variant mellows the intense heat by incorporating beetroot and carrots for natural sweetness and body, while red plum sauce (tkemali) provides the quintessential Georgian tartness that balances the richness of the vegetable puree.

## Ingredients:
> - 1 kg red hot chili peppers
> - 1 kg beets (root)
> - 1 kg carrots
> - 300 g peeled garlic
> - 1/2 liter red Tkemali (plum) sauce
> - 1/2 kg (450 g) tomato paste
> - 1 cup oil
> - 1 cup vinegar
> - 3 bunches dill
> - 3 bunches celery leaves (lovage)

## Instructions:
> - Pass all the ingredients through a blender.
> - Bring the mixture to a boil in an aluminum or non-stick pot.
> - Add salt to taste, be careful not to let it burn!
> - Simmer for approximately 15-20 minutes, until the bright color of the herbs begins to change.
> - It is recommended that as soon as you turn off the heat, immediately add 3 ground aspirin tablets and stir well.
> - Pour the hot mixture into jars (previously sterilized and dried) and seal them tightly.

## Notes:
> - Continuous stirring is vital during the short boiling phase to prevent the thick vegetable base from scorching the bottom of the pot. Adding ground aspirin is a traditional regional preservation technique that helps stabilize the sauce for long-term storage without the need for prolonged boiling that would dull the fresh flavors.
