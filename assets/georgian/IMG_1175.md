# Chocolate Cake

This traditional Georgian chocolate cake utilizes a "boiled" dough technique and a rich custard-based "creme" filling. The method of heating the liquids with sugar and fat before sifting in the flour ensures a moist, dense crumb characteristic of Eastern European home baking, while the custard cream (similar to Ermine frosting) provides a smooth, less sugary finish than standard American buttercreams.

## Ingredients:

> **For the Cake Base:**
> * 4 eggs
> * 100g sugar
> * 1 cup warm water
> * 100g butter (or margarine)
> * Cocoa powder (enough to melt into the mixture)
> * 1 cup additional sugar
> * 1 packet vanilla
> * 220g flour (sifted)
>
> **For the Cream (Frosting):**
> * 1/2 liter milk
> * 1 1/2 cups sugar
> * A pinch of salt
> * 50g flour + 1 1/2 cups flour (for thickening)
> * 1 cup water (for flour slurry)
> * 175g butter (divided: 125g and 50g)
> * 2 egg yolks
> * 1 packet vanilla
> * Cocoa powder

## Instructions:

> **To Prepare the Cake Base:**
> 1. Pour 1 cup of warm water into a pot and bring it to a boil. 
> 2. Once boiling, drop in 100g of butter and the cocoa powder so it melts. Add 1 cup of sugar and 1 packet of vanilla, then mix well.
> 3. Boil the mixture for 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and let it cool for 1 hour, beating it with a mixer as it cools.
> 4. Mix in 1 to 1 1/2 ladles of flour, then sift in the remaining 220g of flour. 
> 5. Incorporate the 4 egg whites (beaten until they rise) by folding them in from the top. 
> 6. [Bake at] 200-250 [degrees/setting]. Once baked, take it out and let it rest for 3-5 minutes before removing from the pan.
>
> **To Prepare the Cream:**
> 1. Place 1/2 liter of milk on low heat. 
> 2. Separately, in a glass or bowl, mix 1 1/2 cups of flour with 1 cup of water until completely smooth. 
> 3. Slowly stir this flour slurry into the warming milk and whisk well. Continue to heat until the mixture thickens into a heavy custard.
> 4. Once the mixture has cooled down, add 125g of butter. 
> 5. Add an additional 50g of butter and 2 egg yolks, mixing well. Add the vanilla. 
> 6. Finally, add the cocoa and beat with a mixer until the cream is fully incorporated and light (do this slowly if the mixture is still slightly warm).

## Notes:

* **Temperature Control:** Ensure the boiled chocolate base is sufficiently cooled before adding the eggs and flour to avoid curdling or premature rising.
* **Cream Texture:** When making the flour slurry for the cream, ensure there are absolutely no lumps before adding it to the milk for a silky finish.
* **Whipping:** For the best volume, beat the egg whites to stiff peaks before folding them into the base.
