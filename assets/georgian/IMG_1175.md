# Zeinab's Shu


## Ingredients:

> **For the Puff Base:**
> * 4 eggs
> * 100 g butter
> * 1 cup warm water
> * Salt
> * 1 cup sifted flour

>
> **For the Cream (Frosting):**
> * 1 liter milk
> * 1 1/2 cups sugar
> * A pinch of salt
> * 50g butter + 1 cup flour 
> * 1 cup water 
> * 1 egg yolk


## Instructions:

> **To Prepare the Cake Base:**
> 1. Pour 1 cup of  water into a pot add salt and bring it to a boil. 
> 2. Once boiling, drop in 100g of butter and bring to a slow simmer. 
> 3. When butter melts, add 1 cup of sifted flour, constantly stirring on low heat
> 4. After 1-2 minutes, turn off the heat and mix in using a mixer 4 eggs 
> 5. Using Nino's teas spoon, scoop 1 or 1 1/2  tsp of mixure and lay it out on a baking sheet
> 6. Heat the oven to 220 C (425 F). Once they start puffing up and rising, turn the oven down to 200 C.
> 7. And once ready, turn off the heat and leave for 3-5 minutes before removing from the oven.
>
> **To Prepare the Cream:**
> 1. Place 1/2 liter of milk on low heat. 
> 2. Separately, in a glass or bowl, mix 1 1/2 cups of flour with 1 cup of water until completely smooth. 
> 3. Slowly stir this flour slurry into the warming milk and whisk well. Continue to heat until the mixture thickens into a heavy custard.
> 4. Once the mixture has cooled down, add 125g of butter. 
> 5. Add an additional 50g of butter and 2 egg yolks, mixing well. Add the vanilla. 
> 6. Finally, add the cocoa and beat with a mixer until the cream is fully incorporated and light (do this slowly if the mixture is still slightly warm).

## Notes:

* **Temperature Control:** Ensure the boiled chocolate base is sufficiently cooled before adding the eggs and flour to avoid curdling or premature rising.
* **Cream Texture:** When making the flour slurry for the cream, ensure there are absolutely no lumps before adding it to the milk for a silky finish.
* **Whipping:** For the best volume, beat the egg whites to stiff peaks before folding them into the base.
