# Zainab's Khachapuri

Matsoni (Georgian fermented milk) provides natural acidity that reacts with baking soda to leaven the dough, while the process of spreading, greasing, and layering with soda creates a characteristically soft and slightly flaky texture without the need for yeast.

## Ingredients

* 800 g Matsoni
* 200 g Butter
* 6 cups Flour
* 1/2 tsp Soda
* Salt

## Instructions

1. Place the flour on a board
2. Mix it, make a crater in the middle, add matsoni
3. Mix it with a spatula, then by hand.
4. Sprinkle baking soda bit by bit 3 times.
5. Do not squash the dough
6. Roll it into a sausage shape and divide into 3 parts.

## Notes

* Matsoni can be substituted with plain whole-milk Greek yogurt if unavailable; ensure it is at room temperature to react properly with the soda.
* Avoid over-kneading the dough after adding the soda to maintain the delicate air pockets that create a light texture.
