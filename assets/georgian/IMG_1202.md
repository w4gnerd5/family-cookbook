# Zainab's Khachapuri

Matsoni (Georgian fermented milk) provides natural acidity that reacts with baking soda to leaven the dough, while the process of spreading, greasing, and layering with soda creates a characteristically soft and slightly flaky texture without the need for yeast.

## Ingredients
> 800.0 g Matsoni (fermented milk/yogurt)
> 200 g Butter
> 6 cups Flour
> 1/2 tsp Soda (with vinegar)

## Instructions
> Divide, divide 3.
> Make a soft dough.
> Spread it out, then grease it by hand.
> Sprinkle the soda bit by bit 3 times.
> Do not squash it; keep it in the cold.
> Divide the khachapuri into 3 parts.

## Notes
- Matsoni can be substituted with plain whole-milk yogurt if unavailable; ensure it is at room temperature to react properly with the soda.
- Avoid over-kneading the dough after adding the soda to maintain the delicate air pockets that create a light texture.
