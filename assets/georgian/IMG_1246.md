# Christmas Salad

This hearty Slavic holiday salad features a classic combination of sautéed mushrooms, onions, and boiled protein, layered or mixed with tangy mayonnaise and crunchy rye bread croutons, showcasing the rich, savory profiles typical of festive Russian winter tables.

## Ingredients:
> *   Hard-boiled eggs – 5 pcs.
> *   Boiled chicken – 1 kg (or boiled beef).
> *   Onions – 3 pcs.
> *   Mushrooms (Champignons) – 800g.
> *   Mayonnaise – 500g.
> *   Croutons (small cubes of black bread) – 1/2 loaf.

## Instructions:

## Notes:
Sauté the mushrooms and onions until they are deeply golden to ensure a rich umami flavor. If the recipe cuts off, typically these ingredients are layered or tossed with the remaining boiled eggs and mayonnaise, then topped with the black bread croutons just before serving to maintain their crunch.
