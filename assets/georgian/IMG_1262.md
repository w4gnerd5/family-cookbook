# Dough Preparation and Baking Instructions

This traditional Georgian baking technique emphasizes the importance of double-rising and temperature control to ensure a light, airy texture, which is a hallmark of festive breads like *Paska*. The second proofing inside the mold and the final egg wash are critical steps for achieving the desired height and a glossy, golden-brown finish.

## Ingredients
> *The original text focuses exclusively on the preparation and baking process; specific ingredients for the dough itself are not listed in this excerpt.*

## Instructions
> Place the dough in a warm spot. Once it has risen, knead it thoroughly and place it back in a warm area once again.
> 
> After the second rise, distribute the dough into baking molds so that it fills only half of each mold.
> 
> Leave small gaps and let the dough rest in the molds for a short while until it rises further, after which you should brush the top with egg yolk.

## Notes
To ensure the best results, avoid applying an excessive amount of egg yolk to the top of the dough, as this can lead to burning. Use a wooden skewer or stick to gently press along the sides of the mold to help the bread release cleanly and prevent the edges from scorching.
