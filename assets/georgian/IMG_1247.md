# Bird's Nest and Chicken Salad

These recipes showcase classic Soviet-era festive preparations where simple root vegetables and proteins are transformed through specific textures and creative plating. The "Bird's Nest" presentation is a popular culinary tradition that uses croutons to mimic a nest and peeled eggs as a visual centerpiece, relying on the contrast between crunchy bread and a creamy, mayonnaise-bound base for its piquant flavor.

## Ingredients
> * Black pepper, salt
> * Coarsely grated carrots
> * Mayonnaise
> * Black bread croutons (cubes)
> * Peeled eggs
> * Red radish, shredded (shavings)
> * Boiled chicken, finely chopped
> * Onion, sautéed in vegetable oil
> * Black pepper, salt
> * Mayonnaise

## Instructions
> One hour before serving, add the black bread croutons. Serve the salad in the shape of a nest—make a hollow in the center and place peeled eggs inside—and we get a "bird's nest with eggs." This is very tasty and piquant. 
> 
> For the Chicken Salad: Combine the shredded red radish, finely chopped boiled chicken, and onion sautéed in vegetable oil. Season with black pepper and salt, then mix with mayonnaise.

## Notes
> To maintain the proper texture, ensure the bread croutons are added exactly one hour before serving so they stay crisp. For the radish, it is best to cut it into very fine "shavings" to ensure it blends well with the tender chicken.
