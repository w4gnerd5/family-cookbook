# Pan-Fried Flatbread Instructions

The use of baking soda "slaked" (quenched) with vinegar is a fundamental chemical leavening technique in Slavic baking, providing an immediate lift and an airy texture to doughs and batters without the need for yeast. Dry-frying on a skillet without oil is a traditional method for preparing specific types of flatbreads or thick pancakes, resulting in a clean, toasted flavor that pairs perfectly with savory fillings.

## Ingredients
> * 1/2 tsp baking soda
> * Vinegar (to slake the soda)

## Instructions
> And when you have mixed all of this, add 1/2 tsp of baking soda slaked with vinegar.
>
> Fry on a frying pan without oil.
>
> It is tastier with meat.
>
> — Yu. M.

## Notes
To "slake" the soda, place the powder in a small spoon and add just enough vinegar to make it fizz vigorously before stirring it into the mix; this prevents a metallic aftertaste. Use a well-seasoned cast iron or a non-stick pan to ensure the dough releases easily without the use of fat.
