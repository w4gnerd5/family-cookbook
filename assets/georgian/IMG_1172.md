# Gia's Khachapuri Recipe

Khachapuri is Georgia's quintessential cheese bread, a staple of national identity that balances a soft, yeasted dough with a rich, molten cheese filling. The science of the recipe relies on the interaction between the high-moisture Imeretian and Sulguni cheeses, which provide the perfect elasticity and tangy flavor profile when baked at a high temperature.

## Ingredients
> * Water: 300.0
> * Yeast: 2–3 tsp (5.0)
> * Salt
> * Sugar: a bit more
> * A little milk
> * Flour: 300.0 to 450.0 (approx. 1 1/2 to 2 cups)
> * Sunflower oil: 6–7 spoons (for greasing hands)
> * Additional oil: 30.0–40.0
> * Cheese (for 1 khachapuri): 300.0
> * Imeretian cheese: 900.0 (for the batch)
> * Sulguni cheese: 200.0
> * Eggs: 2 pieces (to be mixed into the cheese)

## Instructions
> 1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar together.
> 2. Add a little milk to the mixture.
> 3. Add flour (starting with 300g and going up to 450g) and a little salt and oil.
> 4. Knead the dough. Grease your hands with 6–7 spoons of oil to prevent sticking.
> 5. If the dough starts sticking to your hands during kneading, add 30.0–40.0 of oil.
> 6. Once the dough has risen, sprinkle it with flour.
> 7. Prepare the filling by mixing the Imeretian and Sulguni cheeses with 2 eggs.
> 8. Divide the dough into portions (approx. 300g of cheese per khachapuri).
> 9. Shape the dough into circles and enclose the cheese filling.
> 10. Do not skimp on the oil when shaping.
> 11. Bake for approximately 10 minutes.

## Notes
To achieve the traditional texture, ensure the dough remains very soft and slightly tacky before rising. Using a mix of Imeretian cheese for saltiness and Sulguni for meltability is essential for the authentic "cheese pull" effect.
