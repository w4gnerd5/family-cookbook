# Vegetable Enchilada Casserole

This dish is a hearty vegetarian take on traditional enchiladas, layering classic Tex-Mex flavors into a convenient casserole format. The combination of beans, corn, and zucchini provides a satisfying texture, while layering the tortillas instead of rolling them ensures every bite is thoroughly infused with sauce and melted cheese.

## Ingredients:
> * Oil
> * Onions
> * Zucchini
> * Beans
> * Tomatoes
> * Corn
> * Cumin & Salt
> * Enchilada sauce
> * Tortillas
> * Cheese

## Instructions:
> 1. Sauté onions in oil, add zucchini, beans, tomatoes, corn, cumin & salt.
> 2. Cook til hot.
> 3. In 9X13 pan : Layer —
>    1. Enchilada sauce
>    2. Corn tortillas
>    3. 1/2 vegetables
>    4. Cheese
>    5. Repeat — end cheese
> 4. Cover with foil.
> 5. Bake 400°.
> 6. 15 min covered.
> 7. 15 min — uncovered.

## Notes:
> To ensure the casserole sets properly, make sure the vegetable mixture is steaming hot before layering. Removing the foil for the final half of the baking time is essential for achieving a bubbly, golden-brown cheese topping.
