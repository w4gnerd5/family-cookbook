# Georgian Khachapuri (Cheese Bread)

This recipe details the preparation of a traditional Georgian Khachapuri, a staple cheese-filled bread. The core science of this recipe relies on a high-hydration, enriched yeast dough that is briefly rested to develop gluten elasticity, paired with a high-heat bake (220-250°C) to ensure a rapid rise and a characteristic golden, soft crust that encases the molten cheese interior.

## Ingredients

> * **Dough:**
>   * Warm water: 1/2 cup (or up to 300ml for a larger batch)
>   * Yeast: ~ 5.0 g
>   * Salt: 3.0 g
>   * Sugar: 1/2 teaspoon
>   * Flour: ~ 1/2 kg (500g)
>   * Vegetable oil: 30–40 g
> * **Filling:**
>   * Curd cheese (Khacho): 300 g
>   * Grated cheese (Sulguni or Imeretian): 200 g
>   * Water: A small amount to loosen the cheese mixture
>   * Flour: 1 tablespoon (to bind the filling)
> * **Topping:**
>   * 1 Egg (beaten)
>   * A handful of extra grated cheese

## Instructions

> 1. Mix the warm water, yeast, salt, sugar, and flour. Knead the dough until it is smooth and soft. Incorporate the oil at the end of kneading. Let the dough rest and rise for approximately 40 minutes.
> 2. Once the dough has risen, divide it into portions (about the size of 1 cup each). 
> 3. Prepare the filling by mixing the curd cheese and coarsely grated cheese. Add a little water to soften the mixture and a spoonful of flour to bind it.
> 4. Flatten a piece of dough with your hands. Place a generous amount of cheese filling in the center.
> 5. Gather the edges of the dough over the filling and pinch them together at the top to seal it completely (forming a bundle).
> 6. Turn the bundle over so the seam is on the bottom. Gently flatten the dough again with your hands into a round or oval shape, being careful not to tear the dough.
> 7. Let the prepared breads rest for about 10 minutes before baking.
> 8. Brush the top with a mixture of beaten egg and a little bit of cheese.
> 9. Bake in a preheated oven at 220–250°C (430–480°F) until the crust is golden brown.

## Notes

* **Temperature:** Ensure the oven is very hot (at least 220°C) before sliding the bread in; this high heat is vital for the dough's "oven spring."
* **Texture:** The filling should be moist but not runny; add water to the cheese gradually until it reaches a spreadable consistency.
* **Elasticity:** If the dough resists flattening, let it rest for 5 minutes to allow the gluten to relax.
