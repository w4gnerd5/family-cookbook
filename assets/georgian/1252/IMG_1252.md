# Gozinaki Proportions

Gozinaki is a traditional Georgian New Year's confection made of walnuts and honey. Its success depends on carefully heating the honey to a specific concentration so that it binds the nuts into a crisp, snap-able brittle without scorching or losing its characteristic pale golden color.

## Ingredients

> * 1 tea cup of honey
> * 1 tablespoon of sugar
> * 6 cups of chopped walnuts

## Instructions

> * Mix 1 tea cup of honey and 1 table spoon of sugar on low heat.
> * Keep stirring constantly with a spoon. Do not let it burn!
> * When you first place it on the stove, it will foam up —immediately remove it from the heat.
> * After a short while, place it back on low heat and bring it to a boil.
> * Test the readiness by dropping a bead into a glass of cold water to see how it falls.
> * Take 6 cups of un-chopped walnuts.
> * Cover them with the mixure and then cut them finely after it cools off.

## Notes

> * The cold water test is essential: the honey is ready when a drop forms a firm ball that does not dissolve in the water.
> * Removing the honey from the heat when it first foams prevents bitterness and maintains the light color.
