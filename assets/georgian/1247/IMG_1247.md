# Chicken Salad

A five-line notebook salad of shredded red radish and boiled chicken, bound with mayonnaise and sweetened by onion sautéed in oil. The radish keeps its bite against the soft chicken, a quick everyday counterpoint to the festive salads on the neighboring pages.

## Ingredients

* Red radish, shredded (shavings)
* Boiled chicken, finely chopped
* Onion, sautéed in vegetable oil
* Black pepper, salt
* Mayonnaise

## Instructions

1. For the Chicken Salad: Combine the shredded red radish, finely chopped boiled chicken, and onion sautéed in vegetable oil. Season with black pepper and salt, then mix with mayonnaise.
