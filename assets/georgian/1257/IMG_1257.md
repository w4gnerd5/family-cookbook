# Red Tkemali (Plum Sauce)

Red Tkemali is a quintessential Georgian condiment made from tart cherry plums, serving as a vibrant, tangy accompaniment to grilled meats and roasted potatoes. The sauce's signature profile relies on the high acidity of the fruit, which is traditionally balanced by fresh aromatic herbs and the warm, earthy notes of *khmeli suneli*, a classic Georgian spice blend.

## Ingredients
> * 1 kg red cherry plums (Tkemali)
> * 1/2 cup (100g) water
> * 4-5 cloves garlic (chopped)
> * 1 small red chili pepper (finely chopped)
> * 5 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
> * 3 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
> * 2 teaspoons ground coriander (dried)
> * 2 teaspoons khmeli suneli
> * 2 teaspoons salt (or to taste)
> * 1 tablespoon sugar (if the plums are very sour)

## Notes
To achieve the traditional consistency, the plums should be simmered until soft and then pressed through a sieve to remove pits and skins before adding the remaining ingredients. Only add the sugar at the very end of cooking, after tasting, as the natural sweetness of red plums can vary greatly depending on their ripeness.
