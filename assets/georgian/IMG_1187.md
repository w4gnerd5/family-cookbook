## Notes
The handwritten Georgian uses colloquialisms; specifically, "გამიხურდა" (gamikhurda) literally translates to "heated up," but idiomatically means becoming frustrated or impatient. The word "ყური" (quri) literally means "ear," but in this context refers to the handle of a cup depicted in the sketch.
