# Samsa (Flaky Meat Pastries)

Samsa is a beloved savory pastry across Central Asia and the Caucasus, distinguished by its incredibly flaky, laminated crust and juicy meat filling. The technique of rolling dough paper-thin, layering it with melted butter, and coiling it into a tight roll creates the signature "puff" effect, while the addition of sparkling mineral water to the meat prevents the filling from drying out during the high-heat bake.

## Ingredients

> **For the Dough:**
> * 200 ml water
> * 1 tsp salt
> * 1 egg
> * 500g flour
> * Melted butter (for brushing)
>
> **For the Filling:**
> * 300g meat (finely chopped)
> * Onions
> * 2 potatoes
> * Salt, pepper, and cumin to taste
> * 50 ml sparkling mineral water

## Instructions

> 1. Mix 200 ml water, 1 tsp salt, 1 egg, and 500g flour. Knead into a stiff dough and let it rest for 30 minutes.
> 2. Divide the dough into 4 equal parts and shape them into balls.
> 3. Roll out each ball into a very thin sheet.
> 4. Brush each rolled-out sheet with melted butter and stack all four layers on top of each other.
> 5. Press the layers together to remove any trapped air, then roll them up tightly into a long log (roulade).
> 6. Cut the log into equal pieces.
> 7. Place the dough pieces in the refrigerator to chill for 1 hour.
> 8. Prepare the filling: Combine the finely chopped meat, onions, 2 chopped potatoes, salt, pepper, cumin, and 50 ml of mineral water.
> 9. Roll out the chilled dough pieces into circles, add the filling, and fold as desired.
> 10. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40 minutes.

## Notes

* **Texture Control:** It is vital to roll the dough layers as thin as possible; the thinner the sheets, the more delicate the lamination will be. 
* **Chilling:** Do not skip the 1-hour refrigeration step for the dough log; it firms the butter between the layers, ensuring a clean cut and a better rise in the oven.
