# Marinated Bell Peppers

This recipe captures the essence of home preservation in the Caucasus, where bell peppers (traditionally called "Bulgarian peppers") are lightly blanched in a seasoned marinade. This technique ensures the peppers retain a crisp texture while absorbing the sharp, aromatic flavors of garlic, vinegar, and fresh parsley, creating a staple side dish for the winter months.

## Ingredients

> * Red and yellow bell peppers
> * A small amount of oil
> * Black peppercorns
> * Bay leaves
> * Vinegar
> * Salt
> * Fresh parsley
> * Garlic (sliced)

## Instructions

> 1. Wash the red and yellow peppers.
> 2. Cut them into quarters or halves.
> 3. Pour a little oil into a pot.
> 4. Add black peppercorns, bay leaves, and vinegar.
> 5. Bring the mixture to a boil and add salt.
> 6. Once boiling, cook the peppers in the mixture for 1-2 minutes.
> 7. Remove the peppers and arrange them in a jar.
> 8. Layer with parsley and garlic sliced in half.

## Notes

> To ensure the peppers stay crisp, do not exceed the 2-minute boiling time; they should be slightly softened but still firm. Packing the peppers tightly in the jar while still hot helps the flavors meld more effectively.
