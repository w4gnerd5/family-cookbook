# Boiling Water Pancakes (Blini)

Boiling water pancakes, known in Eastern Europe as *zavarnye blini*, utilize a "custard" technique where boiling water is whisked into the batter. This process partially gelatinizes the flour's starch, resulting in an exceptionally elastic, tender, and lacy texture with characteristic tiny holes, making them perfect for filling or folding.

## Ingredients

* 1/2 liter milk + 2 eggs
* 2.5 cups flour
* 1 tablespoon sugar
* 1 tsp salt
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 1/2 liter boiling water
* 1 teaspoon baking soda/vinegar

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients together.
2. Slowly pour in 1/2 liter of boiling water while stirring constantly.
3. Add 1 teaspoon of baking soda/vinegar.

## Notes

* To ensure the best texture, whisk the batter vigorously while adding the boiling water to prevent the eggs from scrambling. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes before frying to allow the bubbles to settle and the flour to fully hydrate.
