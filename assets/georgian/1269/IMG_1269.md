# Pirozhki with Kefir

This quick dough recipe utilizes the lactic acid in kefir to help the yeast activate rapidly, creating an exceptionally tender and airy texture ideal for making traditional Russian stuffed buns (pirozhki), whether fried or baked.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup warm kefir
* 2 tablespoons sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 50 ml vegetable oil
* 1 tablespoon yeast
* 2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. - Mix and beat the warm kefir, sugar, salt, vegetable oil, and yeast together with a fork.
2. - Add the flour and knead until a soft dough forms.

## Notes

* Ensure the kefir is only lukewarm (around 100°F/38°C) so as not to kill the yeast; the final dough should be soft and slightly tacky to the touch for the best results.
