# Rusiko's Cutlets

These Georgian-style meat cutlets, known as *kotleti*, are a beloved staple of Caucasian home cooking. This specific variation incorporates boiled buckwheat as a binding agent instead of the traditional heavy use of soaked bread, which introduces a deep, nutty flavor and ensures a moist, tender interior.

## Ingredients:
> * 3 pounds (Ground meat)
> * 1 Egg
> * 1 Large onion
> * 1 Celery stalk (labeled as "Celery" in original)
> * A bit of parsley
> * A bit of Oregano (fresh or dried)
> * A very small amount of Nutmeg
> * 1/2 tsp dry spice (Khmeli Suneli), or as desired
> * A bit of Italian seasoning
> * A bit of Caraway
> * Ground dried red pepper
> * 1–2 Mint leaves
> * 2 tbsp Olive oil
> * Breadcrumbs (1/2 cup to 1 cup, or slightly more as needed)
> * Slightly more than 1/2 cup boiled buckwheat
> * Finely ground black pepper
> * Salt

## Instructions:
> The original document consists of an annotated ingredient list; specific preparation and cooking steps were not provided in the text. Generally, for this type of recipe, the ingredients are thoroughly mixed by hand, formed into oval patties, and pan-fried until browned on both sides and cooked through.

## Notes:
For the best texture, ensure the boiled buckwheat is completely cooled before mixing it into the meat. Add the breadcrumbs gradually until the mixture is firm enough to hold its shape but still remains tacky to the touch.
