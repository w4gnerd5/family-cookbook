# Yeast Dough for Khachapuri, Pies, and Pirozhki

This versatile yeast dough is a staple of Eastern European and Caucasian baking, specifically formulated to produce a soft, enriched crumb suitable for savory filled breads. The inclusion of both butter and sunflower oil provides a unique elasticity and richness, making it the perfect base for traditional Georgian khachapuri or classic Russian hand pies.

## Ingredients

* 1. 0.5 liters – milk
* 2. 200 g – butter or margarine
* 3. 1 packet (5g) – instant yeast
* 4. 1 tbsp. – sugar
* 5. 1 tbsp. – sunflower oil
* 6. 600 g – flour

## Instructions

1. - Heat the milk (so that it is warm).
2. - Melt the butter.
3. - Mix the milk and 1/2 part of the melted butter, then add the other ingredients, except for the yeast (remember, yeast cannot be dissolved in a hot environment! They will not rise!). The environment must be warm; then pour in the yeast and flour and knead. When the dough becomes homogeneous, pour in the other half of the butter and knead. The dough will turn silky and won't stick to your hands. You can also make Gvezeli with this dough

## Notes

* The most critical factor is temperature control: ensure the milk and butter mixture is lukewarm (around 100°F–110°F) before adding the yeast. If the liquid is too hot to touch comfortably, it will kill the yeast, and the dough will not rise.
