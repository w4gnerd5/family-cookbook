# Khachapuri Oven Dough

Khachapuri is the iconic cheese bread of Georgia, varying by region and preparation style. This specific recipe utilizes a "rubbed-in" fat method—similar to shortcrust pastry—to create a flaky, tender dough suitable for baking in an oven. The inclusion of tarragon is a quintessential Georgian touch, providing a distinct anise-like aroma common in Caucasian savory pies.

## Ingredients

> * Flour
> * Butter (to be cubed)
> * Optional filling additions: Cabbage, mushrooms, or tarragon

## Instructions

> 1. Pour flour onto a cutting board. Coat the butter in the flour and cut it into cubes with a knife.
> 2. Continue by crumbling the mixture with your hands until it reaches a homogeneous consistency (small grains of butter should remain visible within the flour).
> 3. Form [a well] in this flour mixture...
> 
> *Note from the header:* Only at the end, ensure there are no holes and no extra oil!
> *Alternative filling tip:* In the pie, you can use mushrooms instead of cabbage—and as you know yourself—tarragon! Ho-ho!

## Notes

* **Temperature Control:** Keep the butter cold while working it into the flour. The "grains" of butter mentioned are essential for creating a flaky texture once the dough hits the heat of the oven.
* **Consistency:** Ensure the dough is sealed well ("without holes") to prevent the steam or cheese from escaping during baking.
