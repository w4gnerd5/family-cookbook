# Vareniki Dough (Dumpling Dough)

This recipe utilizes the "scalded dough" technique, a staple of Eastern European culinary tradition. By pouring boiling water directly into the flour, the starches partially gelatinize, resulting in an exceptionally elastic and pliable dough that can be rolled paper-thin without tearing and maintains a silky, tender texture after boiling.

## Ingredients

> **Method 1:**
> * 500g Sifted flour
> * 200–250g Boiling hot water
> * 1 teaspoon Salt
> * 3 tablespoons Oil
> * 1 or 2 Eggs
>
> **Method 2 (Alternative Proportion):**
> * 100ml Boiling water
> * 30g Oil
> * Salt
> * 1 Egg
> * 280–300g Flour

## Instructions

> **For Method 1:**
> 1. Make a well in the sifted flour.
> 2. Pour the boiling hot water into the center.
> 3. Add a teaspoon of salt and let the mixture cool slightly.
> 4. Add the oil, stir, and then add 1 or 2 eggs.
> 5. Knead the dough.
> 6. Add flour bit by bit until the dough is ready.
>
> **For Method 2:**
> 1. Combine the boiling water, oil, and salt.
> 2. Add a small amount of flour to the hot mixture to cool it down, then add the egg.
> 3. Gradually add the remaining 280–300g of flour.
> 4. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.

## Notes

Ensure the water is truly at a rolling boil when added to achieve the proper "scalded" starch consistency. Always allow the hot water/flour mixture to cool slightly before adding the eggs to prevent them from scrambling. Letting the dough rest for at least 30 minutes is crucial for relaxing the gluten, making it much easier to roll out.
