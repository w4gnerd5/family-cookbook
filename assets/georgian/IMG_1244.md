# Choux Pastry (Shu)

This recipe details the preparation of "Shu," the classic Eastern European adaptation of French choux pastry. The technique relies on "scalding" flour in a mixture of boiling water and fat to gelatinize the starches, which creates a stable structure that traps steam during baking, allowing the dough to puff up into the iconic hollow shells used for profiteroles or eclairs.

## Ingredients:
> - Water – 1 cup (approx. 250ml)
> - Butter – 100g
> - Flour – 1 cup
> - Eggs – 4 pieces

## Instructions:
> - Boil the water and butter together until the butter is completely melted. 
> - Remove the pot from the heat, add the flour, and mix it using a mixer.
> - Beat in the eggs one by one while mixing continuously with the mixer. Once you achieve a homogeneous mass, add a pinch of salt.
> - On a cold baking sheet (greased with a thin layer of fat), portion out the dough using a dessert spoon. Place them in a moderately hot oven and bake at moderate heat.

## Notes:
- **Temperature Control:** Ensure the water and butter reach a full boil before adding the flour to ensure proper starch gelatinization.
- **Egg Incorporation:** Adding eggs one by one is crucial; the dough must fully absorb each egg before the next is added to maintain the correct "dropping" consistency.

### Original Russian Text:
> 
> Шу.
> Вода - 1 ч. стакан 
> масло - 100 гр.
> мука - 1 ч. стакан.
> яйцо - 4 шт.
> вскипятить воду + масло, растопить / переставить с огня + муку и замесить миксером вбить по одному яйца и не перевая месить миксером, когда получим однородную массу, добавив щепотку соли.
> на холодную жаровни - смазанную тонким слоем жира - разложить десертной ложкой тесто, поставить в не очень горячую духовку и печь на среднем огне
