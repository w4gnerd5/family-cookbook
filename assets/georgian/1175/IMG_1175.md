# Zeinab's Shu

Shu is the Georgian take on French choux pastry. Light, hollow puffs filled with a rich milk-and-flour cream. Zeinab's version uses a custard-style cream thickened on the stovetop rather than a whipped filling, giving it a denser, more indulgent character typical of Georgian home baking.

## Ingredients

**For the Puff Base:**
* 4 eggs
* 100 g butter
* 1 cup warm water
* Salt
* 1 cup sifted flour

**For the Cream, Version I (Georgian card):**
* 1 liter milk
* 1 1/2 cups sugar
* A pinch of salt
* 50 g butter + 1 cup flour
* 1 egg yolk

**For the Cream, Version II (notebook):**
* 1 liter milk (pencil annotation halves this to 0.5 l)
* 100 g butter (pencil: 50 g)
* 1 tea glass flour
* 2 tea glasses sugar
* A pinch of salt
* Vanilla
* 1-2 egg yolks, plus the beaten whites

## Instructions

**To Prepare the Cake Base:**
1. Pour 1 cup of  water into a pot add salt and bring it to a boil.
2. Once boiling, drop in 100g of butter and bring to a slow simmer.
3. When butter melts, add 1 cup of sifted flour, constantly stirring on low heat
4. After 1-2 minutes, turn off the heat and mix in using a mixer 4 eggs
5. Using Nino's teas spoon, scoop 1 or 1 1/2  tsp of mixure and lay it out on a baking sheet
6. Heat the oven to 220 C (425 F). Once they start puffing up and rising, turn the oven down to 200 C.
7. And once ready, turn off the heat and leave for 3-5 minutes before removing from the oven.

**To Prepare the Cream, Version I:**
1. Warm up milk
2. Separately, in a glass or bowl, mix 1 1/2 cups of sugar with 1 cup of flour.
3. Slowly stir in the mixture into the warming milk and whisk well. Continue on low heat until the mixture thickens and starts bubbling.
4. Add 50 g of butter and let simmer another 20 minutes on low heat.
5. Pour into a bowl and add 1 egg yolk, while the cream is hot and beat it with a mixer. (Freeze the egg whites)
6. From time to time, fluff the cream with a mixer (you can mix in frozen egg whites).

**To Prepare the Cream, Version II:**
1. On a separate piece of paper, mix the sugar and flour so they distribute evenly.
2. Heat the milk, butter, and the flour-sugar mixture together and mix quickly with a mixer.
3. Lower the heat and, stirring with a spoon, bring the cream to a thick consistency.
4. Cook until the raw flour taste disappears; add the vanilla at the end and remove from the heat.
5. When slightly cooled, add 1-2 egg yolks and mix quickly.
6. Beat the egg whites into a thick foam and fold into the cooled cream. No sugar in the whites.

## Notes

* **Cream Texture:** When making the flour slurry for the cream, ensure there are absolutely no lumps before adding it to the milk for a silky finish.
* **Whipping:** For the best volume, beat the egg whites to stiff peaks before folding them into the base.
* **Two creams:** the family recorded this filling twice — on the Georgian card (Version I) and in Zeinab's notebook in Russian (Version II, with pencil marks halving the milk and butter). Both are printed; both originals appear below.
