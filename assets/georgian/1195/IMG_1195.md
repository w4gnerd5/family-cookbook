# Adjarian Khachapuri

This recipe details the preparation of a traditional Georgian Khachapuri, a staple cheese-filled bread. The core science of this recipe relies on a high-hydration, enriched yeast dough that is briefly rested to develop gluten elasticity, paired with a high-heat bake (220-250°C) to ensure a rapid rise and a characteristic golden, soft crust that encases the molten cheese interior.

## Ingredients

**Dough:**
* Dough needs to rise at least 40 minutes
* Warm water: 1/2 pitcher (300 ml water for 2 khachapuri. Add some milk)
* Yeast: ~ 5 g
* Salt: 3 g
* Sugar: 1/2 g
* Flour: ~ 1/2 kg (for 300 ml of water)
* Vegetable oil: 30–40 g

**Filling:**
* Grated Imeretian/Village Cheese: 400 g
* Grated Sulguni 200 g
* Water: A small amount to loosen the cheese mixture
* Flour: 1 tablespoon (to bind the filling)
* 1 egg

**Topping:**
* Egg yolkes

## Instructions

1. Mix the warm water, yeast, salt, sugar, and flour. Knead the dough until it is smooth and soft. Incorporate the oil at the end of kneading. Let the dough rest and rise for approximately 40 minutes.
2. Once the dough rises, dust the table with some flour and pinch off enough dough for one portion, about the size of a fist.
3. With the circular motion, round the dough into a ball, cover it up and let it rest for 10 minutes
4. Once rested, flatten the ball in the middle. Leave the edges thick and puffy.
5. Turn it over, stretch it into an oval shape. Pick it up and lay it down by folding the edges towards the center, pinch the opposite sides, creating a boat shape
6. Gently stretch it from the bottom towards the sides, keep shaping it like a boat
7. Dust some flour on the baking sheet
8. Add grated cheese, some sulguni on top.
9. Bake at 220-250 Celsius
10. When it's half way baked, scrape the dough off the sides with a fork
11. Add an egg yolk and leave in the oven for another minute

## Notes

* **Temperature:** Ensure the oven is very hot (at least 220°C) before sliding the bread in; this high heat is vital for the dough's "oven spring."
* **Texture:** The filling should be moist but not runny; add water to the cheese gradually until it reaches a spreadable consistency.
* **Elasticity:** If the dough resists flattening, let it rest for 5 minutes to allow the gluten to relax.
