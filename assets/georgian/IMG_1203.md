# Beinazi's Khachapuri

Khachapuri is Georgia's quintessential cheese bread, with this specific version featuring a chemically leavened dough made with *matsoni* (a traditional Georgian fermented yogurt). The technique of folding the dough multiple times with chilled fat creates a quick "puff" texture that mimics laminated pastry, relying on the acidity of the yogurt to activate the baking soda for lift without the need for yeast.

## Ingredients
> * 800g Matsoni (Georgian yogurt)
> * 200g Butter
> * 6 cups Flour
> * 1/2 tsp Baking soda (plus salt)

## Instructions
> * Cut and chop (the butter into the flour).
> * Knead into a soft dough. Mix [the soda] into the matsoni.
> * Then spread it over [the dough].
> * Add the soda bit by bit. Fold the dough 3 times.
> * Do not squeeze or crush the dough; keep it in a cold place.
> * Divide into 3 parts.

## Notes
To achieve the desired flakiness, ensure the butter and matsoni remain cold throughout the process. It is critical not to "squeeze" or overwork the dough during the folding stages, as this preserves the air pockets created by the baking soda and maintains the distinct layers of the pastry.
