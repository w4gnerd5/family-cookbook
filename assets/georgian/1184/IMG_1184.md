# Russian Pirozhki with Scalded Yeast Dough (Yields 30)

This traditional recipe utilizes the "scalded" (zavarnoy) method, where boiling water is poured over a small portion of the flour to gelatinize the starches. This technique creates a dough that is exceptionally soft, easy to handle without sticking, and results in a finished pastry that stays tender and fresh much longer than standard yeast breads.

## Ingredients

**Stage I:**
* 1 tsp Salt
* 1 cup Boiling hot water
* 3 tbsp Flour
* 1 tbsp Sugar
* 3 tbsp Vegetable oil

**Stage II:**
* 17.5 g Yeast
* 1 cup Warm (not hot) water
* 500 g Flour

## Instructions

1. **Stage I:** Combine flour (3 tbsp), vegetable oil (3 tbsp), sugar (1 tbsp), and salt (1 tsp). Pour the boiling water over the mixture. Mix thoroughly and let it cool until it is warm to the touch.
2. **Stage II:** To the warm mixture, add the yeast and 1 cup of warm water. Gradually incorporate the 500g of flour. Knead the dough until smooth.
3. **Shaping and Cooking:** Form the dough into small balls. Let the balls rest for 5–10 minutes. Shape the pirozhki with your desired filling and fry them in a skillet with oil. Alternatively, they can be baked in the oven.

## Notes

* The most critical step is ensuring the scalded flour mixture has cooled down to a lukewarm temperature before adding the yeast; if the mixture is too hot, it will deactivate the yeast and the dough will not rise. For a perfect fry, ensure the oil is hot before adding the pirozhki to prevent them from becoming greasy.
