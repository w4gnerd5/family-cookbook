# Camca - Sumsa

Samsa is a staple savory pastry of Central Asian cuisine, traditionally baked in a tandoor but adapted here for a standard oven. The key to its signature flaky, multi-layered texture is the process of rolling the dough extremely thin, layering it with fat (melted butter), and chilling the resulting roll to ensure the layers remain distinct and crisp during the bake.

## Ingredients

> **For the Dough:**
> * 200g water
> * 1 tsp salt
> * 1 egg
> * 500g flour
> * Melted butter (for layering)
>
> **For the Filling:**
> * 300g meat, finely chopped
> * Onions
> * 2 potatoes
> * Salt and spices
> * Oil
> * 50ml sparkling mineral water

## Instructions

> 1. Knead the dough and let it rest for 30 minutes.
> 2. Divide the dough into 4 parts and shape them into balls.
> 3. Roll out each ball until it is very thin.
> 4. Brush each rolled-out layer with melted butter and stack all four layers on top of each other.
> 5. Press out any trapped air and roll the stacked layers into a tight log.
> 6. Cut the log into thin, equal pieces and flatten them out.
> 7. Place the pieces in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
> 8. Prepare the filling by mixing the finely chopped meat, onions, two potatoes, salt, spices, a bit of oil, and 50ml of sparkling water.
> 9. Make small balls and let them rest. Flatten each ball and put the filling in the center. Leave the edges clean. Pinch it like khinkali and leave a hole open.
> 10. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 40 minutes.

## Notes

* **Texture Control:** Do not skip the 1-hour refrigeration step; it hardens the butter between the layers, which is essential for achieving the spiral, flaky "puff" effect.
* **Juiciness:** Finely chopping the meat by hand rather than using a grinder, combined with the addition of sparkling water, ensures the interior remains exceptionally juicy.
