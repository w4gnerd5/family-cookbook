# Melinka's Khachapuri

Khachapuri is the iconic national dish of Georgia, a savory cheese-filled bread that is a staple of Caucasian hospitality. The soul of the dish lies in the balance between a soft, pillowy dough and a rich, tangy cheese filling—traditionally a blend of Imeretian and Sulguni cheeses—which provides the characteristic saltiness and elastic melt-factor that defines the perfect khachapuri.
