# Lyudmila's Beef Stroganoff

Lyudmila’s Beef Stroganoff reflects the historic culinary exchange between Russian and Georgian traditions. While the classic Russian dish typically features a sour cream-based sauce, this version incorporates regional Caucasian preferences for bell peppers and a tomato base, using a starch slurry to achieve the dish's signature silky consistency without heavy dairy.

## Ingredients

> *   Meat (beef), cut thinly and tenderized
> *   Onion
> *   Mushrooms
> *   Salt and pepper
> *   Bell pepper, finely chopped
> *   Grated fresh tomatoes
> *   Canned tomatoes
> *   Ham broth (or juice)
> *   Flour
> *   Starch (1 teaspoon)
> *   Cold water (1/2 cup)
> *   Oil

## Instructions

> 1. Cut the meat into thin strips and flatten/tenderize each piece. 
> 2. Simmer the meat in oil (without added pan lid). 
> 3. Add the onion and optionally mushrooms; season with salt and pepper.
> 4. Simmer the meat again with the added ingredients.
> 5. Add the finely chopped bell pepper.
> 6. Continue simmering the meat.
> 7. Add grated fresh tomatoes and canned tomatoes. Add a small amount of flour (half teaspoon) and stir.
> 8. At the end, prepare the thickener: dissolve 1 teaspoon of starch 1/2 cup of cold water.
> 9. Pour the mixture into the pan and bring the sauce to a boil to thicken. Turn off heat when sauce begins to boil.

## Notes

To ensure the best texture, tenderize the meat thoroughly before slicing to break down fibers. When adding the starch slurry, stir constantly to prevent lumps and stop as soon as the sauce reaches a boil to maintain a glossy finish.
