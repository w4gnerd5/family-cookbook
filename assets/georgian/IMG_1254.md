# American Kebab

This recipe blends traditional kebab preparation with Western-style extra-lean beef and a specific aromatic profile including thyme and rosemary. Using garlic juice instead of minced cloves is a culinary technique that ensures the flavor is evenly distributed throughout the meat without introducing fibrous textures, resulting in a more refined finish.

## Ingredients:
> - 3 packets of 5% fat Extra Lean Ground Beef (Butcher Shop quality)
> - Thyme (Spice Islands brand)
> - Rosemary (Private Selection brand)
> - Coriander (Private Selection brand)
> - Nutmeg
> - Approximately 1/2 teaspoon black onion (likely refers to black garlic or a charred onion seasoning)
> - 1 tablespoon garlic juice

## Instructions:
> - Combine the 5% extra lean ground beef with the listed spices: thyme, rosemary, coriander, and nutmeg.
> - Incorporate the black onion and the tablespoon of garlic juice into the meat mixture, ensuring everything is well-distributed.

## Notes:
Because 5% lean beef is used, it is important to handle the meat gently to avoid a tough texture. The use of garlic juice is essential for a smooth, uniform savory flavor that doesn't overpower the delicate herbal notes of the rosemary and thyme.
