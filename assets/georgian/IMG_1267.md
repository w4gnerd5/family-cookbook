# Pirozhki with Scalded Dough

This recipe utilizes the "zavarnoy" (scalded) method, where boiling water is used to gelatinize a portion of the flour's starch. This technique results in a dough that is exceptionally soft, elastic, and easy to work with, while ensuring the finished pastries stay fresh and tender for a longer period.

## Ingredients

> * 1 teaspoon salt
> * 1 cup boiling water
> * 3 tablespoons flour (for the scald)
> * 1 tablespoon sugar
> * 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
> * 17.5 grams yeast
> * 500 grams flour 

## Instructions

> **Stage 1:** Mix 1 tsp salt, 1 cup boiling water, 3 tbsp flour, 1 tbsp sugar and 3 tbsp vegetable oil. Let the mixture cool. 
> 
> **Stage 2:** Add 17.5 grams of yeast and 500 grams of flour.

## Notes

It is critical to let the scalded mixture from Stage 1 cool until it is lukewarm (approximately 100°F or 38°C) before adding the yeast; if the mixture is too hot, it will kill the yeast and the dough will not rise.
