# Dumpling Dough with Boiling Water (Super)

This traditional "choux" pastry technique for Russian *pelmeni* or *vareniki* uses boiling water to partially gelatinize the starch in the flour. This process results in an exceptionally elastic and pliable dough that can be rolled very thin without tearing, ensuring the dumplings remain intact and tender during the boiling process.

## Ingredients

* 1 egg
* salt
* 3 cups flour
* 1 tbsp. vegetable oil
* 1 cup boiling water

## Instructions

1. Add the salt to the egg and beat with a fork. Then add 3 cups of flour and a spoonful of vegetable oil; mix well. Add 1 cup of boiling water and stir with a spoon, then knead by hand (add a little flour if necessary).

## Notes

* **Temperature Control:** Use water that has just reached a rolling boil to ensure the flour properly scalds, which gives the dough its signature elasticity.
* **Safety:** Start mixing with a spoon immediately after adding the boiling water to avoid burns; only switch to hand-kneading once the temperature is comfortable to touch.
* **Resting:** Although not mentioned in the original note, allowing the dough to rest for 20–30 minutes under a bowl or plastic wrap will significantly improve its texture and make it easier to roll out.
