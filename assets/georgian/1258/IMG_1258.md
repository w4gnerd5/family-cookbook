# Ideal Cake (Ideali)

This Georgian "Ideali" cake is a nostalgic walnut staple, prized for its deep caramelized flavor and soft, layered texture. The recipe relies on honey and baking soda to create tender, aromatic cake layers, while the "cream" is made by slow-boiling sweetened condensed milk to transform it into a rich dulce de leche, a technique that requires careful temperature control to avoid pressure buildup in the can.

## Ingredients

> - 3 eggs
> - 1 cup sugar
> - 2 tablespoons honey
> - 1 teaspoon baking soda (quenched with vinegar)
> - 1 cup ground walnuts
> - 2 cups flour

## Instructions

> Divide the dough into 3 parts and bake 3 layers on parchment paper (the paper must be greased).
>
> **Cream:**
> Boil 1 can of sweetened condensed milk for 2 hours.
> Once cooled, add 200g of softened butter and some chopped walnuts.
>
> During boiling, do not let the water level drop!
> And open the pot lid carefully (so the can doesn't explode)!

## Notes

- **Safety First:** Ensure the can of condensed milk remains fully submerged in water at all times during the 2-hour boil; if the water evaporates, the can may explode. 
- **Assembly:** Allow the boiled condensed milk to cool completely to room temperature before mixing with the butter to prevent the cream from melting or separating.
