# Gia Agirba's Khachapuri Recipe

Khachapuri is Georgia's iconic cheese bread, a staple of hospitality that balances a soft, elastic dough with a rich, salty cheese filling. The science behind the perfect khachapuri lies in the proper fermentation of the yeast dough and the use of high-moisture, young cheeses like Imeretian, which melt into a gooey, savory center during baking.

## Ingredients

> * **Water:** 300.0 ml
> * **Yeast:** 2-3 tsp (approx. 5.0g)
> * **Salt:** To taste
> * **Sugar:** A little extra (for the yeast)
> * **Milk:** A small amount
> * **Flour:** 450.0g to 500.0g (approx. 1/2 kg)
> * **Oil:** Vegetable oil (for kneading)
> * **Cheese:** 900.0g Imeretian cheese (approx. 300.0g per khachapuri)
> * **Eggs:** 2 pieces (to be mixed into the cheese)

## Instructions

> 1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the water with a little milk added.
> 2. Mix in the flour (approx. 450.0g) and knead the dough slightly.
> 3. Let the dough rise for 30-40 minutes until it is airy and no longer sticks to your hands.
> 4. Once risen, knead the dough again with some oil.
> 5. Cover the dough with a clean cloth.
> 6. Prepare the filling by mixing the crumbled Imeretian cheese with 2 eggs.
> 7. When the dough is ready, sprinkle a surface with flour.
> 8. Form the dough into rounds. Be careful not to use too much flour on the bottom; sprinkle it lightly by hand as you pick it up to shape it.
> 9. Stuff the dough rounds with the cheese mixture.
> 10. Let the shaped khachapuri rest for 10 minutes before baking.

## Notes

* **Texture:** The dough should be soft and elastic; adding oil during the second knead helps prevent sticking without making the dough too heavy with flour.
* **Cheese Mix:** If Imeretian cheese is unavailable, a 1:1 mix of low-moisture mozzarella and feta provides a similar texture and saltiness.
* **Resting:** Allowing the dough to rest for 10 minutes after shaping ensures the bread remains tender and airy during baking.
