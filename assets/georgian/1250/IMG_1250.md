# Beef Eggplant / Bell Pepper Boat (Khortsia Tsomshi)

This traditional Georgian preparation transforms tender parboiled meat into a decorative centerpiece by garnishing it with garlic cloves shaped like a boat's sails. The method of "half-boiling" the meat first ensures it remains succulent during the final high-heat bake, while seasoning with fresh herbs and bell peppers provides the aromatic complexity characteristic of Caucasian cuisine.

## Ingredients:
> * Medium-small eggplants
> * Assorted bell peppers (red, green, and yellow; preferably medium-large)
> * Cooking oil
> * Ground beef
> * Salt and pepper
> * Fresh herbs
> * Meat stock (cooking juices)

## Instructions:
> 1. For eggplants that are slightly smaller than medium size, trim off the stems and leaves only at the base. Place them in a pot and lightly fry in oil with the lid on until they become soft.
> 2. Take bell peppers of various colors (red, green, and yellow), these should be medium-large in size. Slice them in half lengthwise, keeping the stems attached so they stay in whole pieces. Fry these as well in oil with the lid on over low heat until they are softened.
> 3. Cook the meat in butter along with onions.
> 4. Once ready, add tomato juice - not tomato paste
> 5. Add salt, black pepper, red or green basil
> 6. Stuff the eggplant and peppers with cooked ground beef
> 7. Give each one a boat shape
> 8. Arrange it into the shape of a boat.
> 9. Cut large garlic cloves in the midde and stick them into the meat so they look like the sails of a boat.
> 10. Bake it for 10–15 minutes before serving.

## Notes:
Use a low heat setting and keep the lid tightly closed during the softening process. This creates a "steam-fry" effect that tenderizes the tough skins of the peppers and eggplants without charring them, which is essential for the desired melt-in-the-mouth texture.
