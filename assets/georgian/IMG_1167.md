# Dough Preparation and Shaping Instructions

In Georgian culinary tradition, the manual handling of dough is essential for achieving the perfect texture in traditional breads and pastries. This note outlines a specific technique for kneading from the center outwards and maintaining moisture to ensure the dough remains pliable for shaping and scoring.

## Ingredients:
> *   Dough (prepared)
> *   Water (for moisture)

## Instructions:
> *   Knead by hand starting from the middle (working towards the edges).
> *   Leave it wet/moist.
> *   Do not place it against the sides.
> *   Knead the dough.
> *   Then, straighten it out lengthwise.
> *   Create two stripes.
> *   Cut along the scored lines.

## Notes:
> Maintaining moisture in the dough is critical for the shaping process; avoid letting the surface dry out. Focus on the manual kneading motion from the center to ensure even distribution and the correct structural integrity for the final stripes and cuts.
