# Czech Baklava

This traditional Georgian pastry, known as "Czech Baklava" (ჩეხური ბაქლავა), relies on the reaction between *matsoni* (Georgian yogurt) and baking soda to create a uniquely tender and crumbly shortcrust. Typically layered with a rich mixture of chopped walnuts and raisins, it represents a classic home-baking style where simple pantry staples are transformed into a textured, festive dessert often served at family gatherings.

## Ingredients

* 4Egg yolks
* 400g Margarine
* 1 cup Matsoni (Georgian yogurt)
* 1 tsp Baking Soda
* 1 tsp Salt
* Vanilla (to taste)
* 4 to 5 cups Flour
* 2 cups Sugar
* 1 cup Chopped Walnuts
* 1 cup Raisins

## Instructions

1. Whisk the margarine until smooth, then add 4 egg yolks and mix well.
2. Combine the matsoni with the baking soda and salt, then add this mixture to the margarine.
3. Gradually incorporate the flour and vanilla until a soft dough forms.
4. Divide the dough into 3 equal portions.
5. Roll out each portion and sprinkle generously with a mixture of sugar, chopped walnuts, and raisins.
6. Layer the dough portions or roll them as desired, then brush the top with the remaining egg yolk.
7. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) until golden brown.

## Notes

* For the most authentic "crunchy" texture, allow the dough to rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before rolling.
* If the dough feels too sticky, add flour gradually; the total amount needed can vary based on the moisture content of the matsoni and the size of the eggs.
