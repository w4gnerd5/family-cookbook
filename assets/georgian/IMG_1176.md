# Bazhe (Georgian Walnut Sauce)

Bazhe is a cornerstone of Georgian celebratory cuisine, prized for its rich, velvety texture and the aromatic depth provided by "Utskho Suneli" (Blue Fenugreek). Traditionally served with poultry, the sauce relies on finely ground walnuts and a precise balance of warm spices to create a creamy emulsion that characterizes the unique flavor profile of the Caucasus region.

## Ingredients

> * 500 g Walnuts
> * 1 dessert spoon Dried Coriander
> * 1/2 dessert spoon Utskho Suneli (Blue Fenugreek)
> * Garlic to taste
> * Chili pepper and salt
> * Yellow flower (Imeretian Saffron/Marigold)

## Instructions

> For 500 grams of walnuts, you need:
> * 1 dessert spoon of dried coriander and 1/2 dessert spoon of utskho suneli. 
> * Add garlic to taste. 
> * Add chili pepper and salt. 
> * Add yellow flower.
> * Add chicken stock or boiled cold water until desired thickness

## Notes

To achieve the authentic silky consistency, walnuts must be ground as finely as possible (ideally passed through a meat grinder twice). Gradually thin the spiced walnut paste with boiled, cooled water until it reaches the consistency of heavy cream.
