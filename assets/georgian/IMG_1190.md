# Royal Ideal Cake (Crunchy Cake)

This traditional Georgian pastry, known as "Khrashuna Namtskhvari" (Crunchy Cake), relies on the reaction between *matsoni* (Georgian yogurt) and baking soda to create a uniquely tender and crumbly shortcrust. Typically layered with a rich mixture of chopped walnuts and raisins, it represents a classic home-baking style where simple pantry staples are transformed into a textured, festive dessert often served at family gatherings.

## Ingredients

> * 5 Eggs (4 yolks used for the dough, 1 reserved for brushing)
> * 400g Margarine
> * 1 cup Matsoni (Georgian yogurt)
> * 1 tsp Baking Soda
> * 1 tsp Salt
> * Vanilla (to taste)
> * 4 to 5 cups Flour (adjust as needed for a soft, workable dough)
> * 2 cups Sugar (plus approx. 1.5 cups for the walnut-raisin mixture)
> * 1 cup Chopped Walnuts
> * 1 cup Raisins

## Instructions

> * Whisk the margarine until smooth, then add 4 egg yolks and mix well.
> * Combine the matsoni with the baking soda and salt, then add this mixture to the margarine.
> * Gradually incorporate the flour and vanilla until a soft dough forms.
> * Divide the dough into 3 equal portions.
> * Roll out each portion and sprinkle generously with a mixture of sugar, chopped walnuts, and raisins.
> * Layer the dough portions or roll them as desired, then brush the top with the remaining egg yolk.
> * Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) until golden brown.

## Notes

> * For the most authentic "crunchy" texture, allow the dough to rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before rolling. If the dough feels too sticky, add flour gradually; the total amount needed can vary based on the moisture content of the matsoni and the size of the eggs.
