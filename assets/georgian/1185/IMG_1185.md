# Ami's Khinkali

Khinkali are the iconic soup dumplings of Georgia, traditionally filled with raw spiced meat that releases a rich, aromatic broth as it boils. The key to authentic flavor is the use of distinct highland herbs like blue fenugreek and summer savory, while a very stiff, cold-water dough is essential to ensure the pleats hold firm and contain the hot juices inside.

## Ingredients

**For the Dough:**
* 4 cups flour
* 1 cup water

**For the Meat Filling:**
* Fresh cilantro
* Some parsley
* Blue fenugreek (Utskho Suneli)
* Summer savory (Khondari)
* Onions
* Garlic
* Black Pepper

1. (Instructions not provided in original — combine meat filling ingredients and mix well with water or broth until soupy. Make a stiff cold-water dough, roll thin, cut circles, fill, and pleat to seal.)

## Notes

* Ensure the dough is worked until very firm to prevent the dumplings from tearing.
* When preparing the meat, mix in a small amount of water or broth to ensure a "soupy" interior once cooked.
