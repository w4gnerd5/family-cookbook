# Zenana’s Khachapuri

This recipe utilizes the traditional "chopped dough" technique, where butter is worked into flour to create distinct layers, resulting in a signature flaky and tender crust. The use of Matsoni (a traditional Georgian yogurt) combined with baking soda creates a light, airy rise and a subtle tang that is characteristic of authentic homemade khachapuri.

## Ingredients

> * 200g butter (softened)
> * 6 cups flour (sifted)
> * 800g Matsoni (Georgian yogurt)
> * 1 teaspoon baking soda
> * Salt to taste

## Instructions

> 1. Beat the softened butter until smooth. 
> 2. Sift 6 cups of flour and pour it over the butter. Use a knife to "chop" the butter into the flour.
> 3. Begin kneading by hand, adding salt as you go.
> 4. Pour in 800g of matsoni and continue chopping the mixture with a knife until it forms a crumbly texture.
> 5. Gradually knead the mixture until you achieve a uniform dough.
> 6. Take the remaining flour and mix it with 1 teaspoon of baking soda.
> 7. Punch the dough down and sprinkle this flour-soda mixture over it a little at a time.
> 8. Repeat the sprinkling and punching process 3 to 4 times; do not pour all the flour-soda mixture in at once.
> 9. Form the dough into balls and divide into 3 equal parts.
> 10. Store the dough in the refrigerator.

## Notes

* **Texture Control:** When "chopping" the dough with a knife, aim for a crumbly, pea-sized texture before adding the matsoni to ensure flakiness.
* **Resting:** Storing the dough in the refrigerator is essential; let it chill for at least an hour to allow the gluten to relax and the fats to firm up before rolling.
