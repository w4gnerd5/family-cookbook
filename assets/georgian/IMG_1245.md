# Custard Cream

This recipe describes a traditional Soviet-style "zavarnoy" (cooked) custard, a staple for filling layered cakes like Napoleon or Medovik. The core technique involves the gelatinization of flour starch to create a thick base, which is then enriched with butter and egg yolks for a silky, stable emulsion.

## Ingredients
> * Bake on medium heat.
> * 1 cup milk
> * 100g butter
> * 1 tea glass flour
> * 2 tea glasses sugar
> * A pinch of salt
> * Vanilla

## Instructions
> * On a separate piece of paper, mix the sugar and flour so that they are distributed evenly.
> * Heat the milk, butter, and the flour-sugar mixture together and mix quickly with a mixer.
> * Lower the heat and, while stirring with a spoon, bring the cream to a thick consistency.
> * Cook until ready (until the raw flour taste disappears); at the end, add the vanilla and remove from the heat.
> * When it has cooled slightly, add 1–2 egg yolks and mix quickly with a mixer. Done!

## Notes
To achieve a professional texture, ensure you cook the mixture long enough to eliminate any graininess or "floury" aftertaste. When adding the egg yolks, ensure the custard has cooled enough to prevent the eggs from scrambling, but remains warm enough to incorporate smoothly.
