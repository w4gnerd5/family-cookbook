# Adjarian Khachapuri

Adjarian Khachapuri is a signature boat-shaped Georgian bread traditionally filled with a mixture of Imeretian and Sulguni cheeses. The dough's elasticity comes from a simple yeast-based foundation, while the high-heat baking process creates a crisp exterior and a molten, cheesy center that is typically finished with a fresh egg yolk and a pat of butter.

## Ingredients

* 10g Yeast + Sugar
* 300ml Warm water
* 5-7g Salt
* 3-4 tbsp Oil
* 450-500g Flour (+ extra 300g for dusting/adjusting)
* 400g Cheese (Sulguni or Imeretian)
* 200g Curd cheese (Khacho)
* 1 Egg (for the filling)

## Instructions

1. Dissolve 10g of yeast with sugar.
2. Add 300ml warm water, 5-7g salt, 3-4 tablespoons of oil, and 450-500g of flour.
3. Knead the dough and let it rest for 30-40 minutes.
4. Once the dough has risen, sprinkle flour on the work surface and remove the dough from the bowl.
5. Using gentle hand movements, take one handful of dough for each khachapuri.
6. Shape the portions into rounds and let them rest for another 10 minutes.
7. Flatten the dough by hand, ensuring the edges remain thick to form the structural walls.
8. Fold along the width and stretch it out to form the boat shape.
9. Mash the cheese and curd cheese together with one egg.
10. Place the filling in the center, fold the sides inward, and pull up the edges at the ends to secure the shape.
11. Bake at 220°C (430°F).

## Notes

* Use lukewarm water (around 100°F) to ensure the yeast activates without dying.
* Avoid over-kneading the dough; it should remain soft and slightly airy for a light crust.
* For the boat shape, leave the perimeter of the dough circle significantly thicker than the center before folding.
