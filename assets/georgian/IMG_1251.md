# Meat in Dough (Khortsia Tsomshi)

This traditional Georgian preparation transforms tender parboiled meat into a decorative centerpiece by garnishing it with dough shaped like a boat's sails. The method of "half-boiling" the meat first ensures it remains succulent during the final high-heat bake, while seasoning with fresh herbs and bell peppers provides the aromatic complexity characteristic of Caucasian cuisine.

## Ingredients:
> * Well-cleaned meat
> * Salt and pepper
> * Fresh herbs
> * Bell peppers
> * Meat stock (cooking juices)
> * Prepared/kneaded dough

## Instructions:
> 1. Clean the meat thoroughly.
> 2. Parboil the meat until it is half-cooked.
> 3. Once ready, cut it into medium-sized slices—do not cut into cubes!
> 4. Season the meat with salt, pepper, herbs, and bell peppers.
> 5. Drench the meat thoroughly in its own cooking liquid.
> 6. Arrange it into the shape of a boat.
> 7. Take a piece of the prepared dough and divide it.
> 8. Cut it into two parts and stick them into the meat so they look like the sails of a boat.
> 9. About 10–15 minutes before serving, place the dish in a hot oven to bake.

## Notes:
For the best results, ensure the oven is preheated to a high temperature so the dough "sails" crisp up quickly without drying out the meat. Slicing the meat into medium strips rather than cubes is vital for the visual "boat" presentation and allows the seasonings to coat the surface more effectively.
