# Zeinab's Khachapuri

Khachapuri is a cornerstone of Georgian cuisine, representing a traditional cheese-filled bread that varies by region. This particular recipe utilizes matzoni, a traditional Caucasian fermented milk product, to create a tender, slightly tangy dough, while the technique of mixing with a knife helps keep the dough light and prevents the butter from over-incorporating or melting from hand heat.

## Ingredients

* 6 cups of flour
* Salt
* 200g soft butter
* 800g matzoni
* 1 level teaspoon of baking soda

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, salt, and soft butter together.
2. Make a well in the mixture.
3. Add 800g of matzoni.
4. Mix everything with a knife and form the dough into a ball.
5. Add 1 tsp of Baking soda and mix it with a knofe/spatula until no flour remains on the board.

## Notes

* If matzoni is unavailable, you can substitute it with thick kefir or full-fat plain Greek yogurt. It is vital to use a knife for mixing rather than your hands initially; this ensures the butter remains cool and the dough stays tender.
