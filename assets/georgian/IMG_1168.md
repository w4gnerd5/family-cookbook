# Traditional Georgian Bread Shaping Instructions

This handwritten note outlines the manual techniques for shaping traditional Georgian dough, likely intended for *Shoti* (long bread) or *Khachapuri*. The method focuses on preserving the dough's structure by flattening it from the center outward and keeping the edges thick, which is essential for achieving the characteristic airy crust and soft interior of authentic Georgian hearth-baked breads.

## Ingredients:
> This document consists of shaping techniques; no specific ingredients are listed in the handwritten text.

## Instructions:
> 1. Flatten the dough by hand, starting from the center.
> 2. Leave the edges thick around the perimeter (do not pat down or flatten the sides).
> 3. Knead the dough.
> 4. Then, flatten it lengthwise.
> 5. Prepare in two different sizes.
> 6. Keep this [note] close at hand.

## Notes:
Hand-shaping instead of using a rolling pin is vital to avoid degassing the dough completely. Leaving the edges untouched allows them to puff up during baking, creating the signature "lip" or border typical of traditional Georgian flatbreads.
