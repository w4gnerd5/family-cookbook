# Boiling Water Vareniki

This recipe utilizes a hot-water dough technique where boiling water is incorporated to partially gelatinize the flour's starches. This process results in an exceptionally pliable, soft, and non-sticky dough that can be rolled very thin without tearing, making it ideal for traditional Slavic filled dumplings.

## Ingredients:
> 4 cups flour  
> 1 cup boiling water  
> 2 eggs (can use 1)  
> Salt  
> Sunflower oil

## Instructions:
> Beat the eggs, oil, and salt together well.  
> Add one cup of boiling water to the mixture.  
> Gradually incorporate the flour until a dough forms.

## Notes:
> To prevent the eggs from curdling, whisk the egg and oil mixture vigorously while slowly pouring in the boiling water. Let the dough rest covered for 20-30 minutes before rolling to allow the gluten to relax for better elasticity.
