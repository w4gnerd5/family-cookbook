# New Seasons Market Cheesy Chilaquiles Casserole

A casserole take on chilaquiles from the New Seasons Market deli: tortilla chips layered with enchilada sauce and cheese, baked just long enough for the edges to soften while the chips in the middle keep their crunch. It lands somewhere between nachos and a casserole, sauced but never soggy.

## Ingredients

* 1 Tbs olive oil
* 1 Med. onion, diced
* 1 Med. Zucchini, diced
* 1 - 15 oz Can Black Beans - rinsed
* 1 - 14 oz Can diced tomatoes - drained
* 1 1/2 C fresh or frozen corn
* 1/2 C canned green chilies - diced
* 1 tsp. Cumin
* 1/2 tsp Salt
* 10-12 corn tortillas - quartered
* 19 oz CAN mild enchilada sauce
* 2 C. Shredded cheese.

## Notes

* To ensure the casserole sets properly, it is vital to thoroughly drain the diced tomatoes and rinse the black beans; excess moisture from these ingredients can prevent the sauce from thickening and result in a soggy texture.
