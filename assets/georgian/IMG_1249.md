# Georgian Aromatic Sauce Base

This recipe describes the preparation of a traditional Georgian aromatic base, essential for sauces like Satsivi or Baje. The key to success is careful emulsification and temperature control; the mixture must be stirred constantly as it reaches a simmer to prevent it from curdling, a process that ensures a smooth, thick consistency once cooled.

## Ingredients

> * Onions (cooked/sautéed)
> * Vegetable oil
> * 1 tablespoon [liquid, typically vinegar or water]
> * Salt (to taste)
> * Imeretian Saffron ("Yellow flower")

## Instructions

> * Once the onions are ready, allow them to cool completely.
> * Move them into a blender.
> * While processing, gradually add walnuts and blended herbs to the blender.
> * Afterward, blend everything together witht the broth —determine the thickness by eye.
> * Keep in mind that the mixture will double  or tripple in thickness after boiling.
> * Do not bring it to a full boil [too rapidly], otherwise it will curdle like cottage cheese.
> * As soon as it begins to simmer, stir it with a spoon.
> * Continue to stir in this manner until walnuts are boiled through.
> * Season with salt to taste; add more if necessary.
> * Finally, add a little white wine vinegar.
> * Stir, bring it to a boil once more, and then remove it from the heat.
> * Once boiled, [let it rest] for cooling...

## Notes

> * **Preventing Curdling:** To ensure the sauce does not "split" or take on a curdled, cottage-cheese-like texture, avoid a vigorous rolling boil. Maintain gentle heat and stir constantly once the mixture warms.
> * **Texture Cues:** The sauce naturally thickens as it cools; the recipe notes it will become twice as thick, so avoid over-reducing it while it is on the stove.
