# Adjarian Khachapuri

Khachapuri is Georgia's quintessential cheese bread, a staple of national identity where the interplay of soft, yeasted dough and melted cheese is paramount. The secret to its texture lies in a high hydration dough enriched with margarine, which creates a tender crumb that perfectly complements the salty, elastic cheese filling.

## Ingredients

> * 1 liter warm water
> * 1 tablespoon yeast
> * 1 tablespoon sugar
> * 100g margarine
> * 1 teaspoon salt
> * Approximately 8 cups of flour (add as much as the dough absorbs)
> * Cheese (for filling)
> * Eggs (for filling and optional topping)

## Instructions

> 1. Take 1 liter of warm water and mix well with 1 tablespoon of yeast, 1 tablespoon of sugar, 100g of margarine, and 1 teaspoon of salt.
> 2. Add the flour (about 8 cups), add just enough so that the dough is not too hard.
> 3. Let the dough rest for 1/2 hour.
> 4. Once it has risen, move the dough to a table and cut it into round balls (this amount makes about 10–11 pieces).
> 5. Let these balls sit until they rise again, then flatten them and give them their final shape.
> 6. Prepare the filling by mixing the cheese with a little water and a beaten egg.
> 7. When the bread is half-baked, you may pour an individual egg into each one (for Adjaruli style).

## Notes

Success depends on dough consistency; it should remain soft and pliable rather than stiff, so add flour gradually. For the filling, adding a splash of water to the cheese ensures it remains creamy and doesn't dry out during the baking process.
