# Oven-Baked Pirozhki

A dough for oven-baked pirozhki, built on a traditional "starter" (preferment). Activating the yeast first with a little flour and warm water gives a strong ferment, and scalding the main flour with boiling water makes the dough soft and pliable, for buns with a light, airy crumb.

## Ingredients

* 3 tablespoons flour
* 1 teaspoon sugar
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 7-8 grams yeast
* 150 ml boiling water
* Separately: 800 grams flour
* 4 tablespoons oil

## Instructions

1. - Mix the first five ingredients (3 tablespoons of flour, sugar, salt, yeast, and warm water) to create a starter.
2. - Separately, mix 800 grams of flour with 2 cups of boiling water.
3. - Mix all ingredients together
4. - Let the dough rest and rise for 40 minutes in a warm place.

## Notes

* **Water Temperature:** Ensure the water is lukewarm (around 100°F / 38°C); water that is too hot will kill the yeast, while cold water will significantly slow down the rise.
* **Dough Consistency:** If the dough feels too sticky after kneading, add flour one tablespoon at a time until it no longer sticks to your hands but remains soft.
