# Vareniki with Boiling Water Dough

This recipe utilizes a scalding technique, where boiling water is used to partially gelatinize the flour's starch. This results in an exceptionally elastic and pliable dough that can be rolled very thin without tearing, ensuring the dumplings hold their shape and stay soft after boiling.

## Ingredients

* 4 cups flour
* 1 cup boiling water
* 2 eggs (can use 1)
* Salt
* Sunflower oil

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs, oil, and salt well. Add one cup of boiling water and then incorporate the flour in parts.

## Notes

* To prevent the eggs from curdling, whisk the egg and oil mixture vigorously while pouring in the boiling water.
* Let the dough rest for 20–30 minutes before rolling to allow the gluten to relax for better elasticity.
