# Chicken, Rice, and Spices Bake

A Food.com potluck favorite: browned chicken thighs baked over basmati rice with turmeric, cumin, curry powder, and a little cinnamon, all cooked in one covered dish with hot chicken stock. As long as the foil seal is tight, the rice comes out right.

## Ingredients

* 4 cups chicken stock
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 8–10 boneless skinless chicken thighs
* 1 medium onion
* 1 garlic clove (chopped, minced or crushed)
* 1/2–3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 1/2 teaspoons ground turmeric
* 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
* 1 1/2 teaspoons curry powder
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional) or 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (optional)
* 3/4–1 teaspoon salt
* 2 cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained, or 2 cups regular medium grain or long-grain rice

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
2. Bring water to boiling and add chicken bouillon cubes; lower heat and simmer until dissolved. Keep it hot.
3. Sprinkle chicken with half the spices (except the garlic).
4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet on med-high to high heat and fry the chicken till both sides are brown; lower heat to medium and remove the chicken to a plate.
5. In the same skillet sauté the onions for 2 minutes, then add the garlic and remaining spices and sauté another 2 minutes; turn off the burner.
6. Add the rice and stir for about 30 seconds to 1 minute, till well coated.
7. Spread rice in the baking dish and place the chicken thighs over the rice.
8. Gently pour in the chicken stock and cover tightly with foil.
9. Bake for 40–45 minutes.

## Notes

* **Author's corrections:** The card notes salt cut to about half and cinnamon reduced to 1/2–3/4 tsp (the site's original 2 tsp is an error).
