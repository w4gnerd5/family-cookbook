# Fresh Huevos Rancheros

Huevos rancheros, or "ranch-style eggs," is a traditional Mexican breakfast that relies on the harmony of textures and temperatures. The foundational science involves lightly toasting or frying the corn tortillas to create a moisture-resistant barrier, ensuring they remain firm under the weight of the salsa and runny egg yolks.

## Ingredients
> **Pico de gallo***
> * 2 medium ripe tomatoes, chopped (about 1 1/2 cups)
> * 1/4 cup finely chopped white onion
> * 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
