# BBQ Rub Recipe

This classic American spice blend utilizes brown sugar to create a caramelized crust (the "bark") on meats while balancing sweet, smoky, and savory notes through a combination of paprikas and aromatics. The inclusion of mustard powder acts as a subtle emulsifier and flavor enhancer, making this a versatile "all-purpose" seasoning for various proteins and vegetables.

## Ingredients
> * 1/2 cup brown sugar
> * 2 tablespoons paprika
> * 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
> * 1 tablespoon black pepper
> * 1 tablespoon kosher salt
> * 1 tablespoon garlic powder
> * 1 tablespoon onion powder
> * 1 teaspoon mustard powder
> * 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions
> 1. Mix all of the ingredients together. Stir in an airtight container until ready to use.
> 2. Spice rub keeps for up to 3 months when stored in a cool, dry place.

## Notes
Ensure the brown sugar is free of large lumps before mixing to guarantee an even distribution of flavor. For the best shelf life, store the rub in a completely dry, airtight glass jar away from direct sunlight to prevent the spices from fading and the sugar from clumping.
