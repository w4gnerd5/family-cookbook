# Baked Salmon with Dill Garlic Beurre Blanc

A Recipe4Living printout for oven-baked salmon fillets with a quick beurre blanc built on a white wine, dill, and garlic reduction mounted with cold butter.

## Ingredients

* 2 lb. salmon, fresh, fillets
* 1/2 cup white wine
* 8 Tbs. butter, cold, cut into 16 pieces
* 1 tsp. dill weed
* 1/2 tsp. garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees. Bake the salmon for 12–15 minutes; the fish should be firm but not dry.
2. Place the wine, dill and garlic in a small saucepan. Reduce on high heat until almost dry.
3. Remove from the heat and quickly add all the butter while whisking briskly.
4. When all the butter has been incorporated, allow to rest 10 minutes. Spoon over the hot salmon.

## Notes

* **Butter:** Keep the butter cold and whisk it in off the heat so the sauce emulsifies instead of breaking.
