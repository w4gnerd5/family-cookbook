# Huevos Rancheros

Huevos Rancheros, or "rancher's eggs," is a traditional Mexican breakfast dish designed to provide sustained energy for a day of physical labor. The recipe’s success relies on the scientific principle of layered textures and balanced acidity: the soft, protein-rich beans and eggs are grounded by a toasted corn tortilla and brightened by the sharp acid of fresh salsa.

## Ingredients

> *   Corn tortillas (certified gluten-free if preferred)
> *   Black beans (referenced in the text)
> *   Salsa (Red salsa or Salsa Verde)
> *   Pico de gallo
> *   Cheese (optional)
> *   Sliced avocado (optional)
> *   Eggs (referenced in the text)

## Instructions

> *   **To modify the flavor profile:** Substitute traditional red salsa and pico de gallo with salsa verde and fresh sliced avocado.
> *   **To prepare as gluten-free:** Ensure that the corn tortillas used are certified gluten-free.
> *   **To prepare as dairy-free:** Simply omit the cheese from the final assembly.
> *   **Note on Nutrition:** The provided nutritional data is an estimate from an online calculator and should not replace professional dietary advice.

## Notes

For the best results, lightly toast the corn tortillas in a skillet until they are pliable and fragrant but not hard. This creates a sturdy base that won't become soggy once the beans and salsa are added.

### Original English Text

**Change it up:** Try salsa verde and sliced avocado instead of red salsa and pico de gallo.

**Make it gluten free:** Be sure to use certified gluten-free corn tortillas.
