# Cashew Nut Loaf

A vegetarian nut loaf from a mid-century community cookbook page (page 43, "Entrees," attributed to Mrs. Ferdinand Welebir). The page is heavily annotated by hand, including a soy sauce substitution for Savita and revised baking directions. The scan also carries four unrelated recipes from the same page, which are not part of this collection.

## Ingredients

* 3 c. raw cashews
* 2 large onions
* 3/4 - 1 c. milk
* 1 1/2 t. paprika
* 1 14-oz. can gluten
* 3/4 c. bread crumbs
* 5 eggs
* 1 T. Savita (annotated: soy sauce)
* 2 T. butter
* 1 T. oil
* 3 T. minced parsley
* 2 t. Bakon yeast
* 1 t. each celery salt, sage, salt

## Instructions

1. Put nuts, gluten and onions through food chopper, coarse blade.
2. Combine all ingredients and mix well.
3. Pour into oiled and crumbed brick-shape tins.
4. Bake covered in pan of water 80 minutes at 350° (annotated: remove from pan of water, uncover and bake 20 min more, or until browned).
5. Serve hot or cold with mushroom or cranberry sauce. Any raw or slightly toasted nuts may be used in place of the cashews. Serves 10-12.

## Notes

* **Handwritten conversions:** back of page notes "2# raw cashews = 6 cups ground."
* **Annotations:** "Savita" is marked "soy sauce" in red; baking time altered from the printed version.
