# Lucky and Rippy's Favorite Dog Food

A homemade boiled chicken-and-rice dog food, kept in the archive for the family dogs. Printed from Allrecipes; the vegetable mix must contain no garlic or onions, which are toxic to dogs.

## Ingredients

* 3 pounds minced chicken meat
* 4 cups rice
* 2 1/2 cups frozen vegetable blend (no garlic or onions), minced
* 6 1/2 cups water

## Instructions

1. Gather all ingredients.
2. Place chicken, rice, and vegetables into a large saucepan or Dutch oven; stir in water until smooth. Place over medium-high heat and bring to a boil; stir constantly.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed, about 25 minutes. Cool completely before serving; store covered in the refrigerator.

## Notes

* **For dogs, not people:** the vegetable blend must contain no garlic or onions.
* **Yield:** combined with kibble, lasts about 4 to 5 days for two large golden retrievers; halve for smaller dogs.
