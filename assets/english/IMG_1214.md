# Turkey Meatloaf

Sauteing the onions and garlic in butter before adding them to the mix is a key scientific step; it softens the harsh sulfur compounds in the aromatics and introduces essential moisture to lean ground turkey, which can otherwise become dry. The combination of breadcrumbs and egg acts as a binder, creating a stable protein-starch matrix that traps juices during the baking process.

## Ingredients
> * 2 tablespoons butter (or margarine)
> * 1 cup onions, chopped
> * 3 cloves garlic, minced
> * 1 1/4 lbs ground turkey
> * 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
> * 1 egg (or 1/4 cup egg substitute)
> * 3/4 cup catsup
> * 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
> * 3/4 teaspoon salt
> * 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions
> 1. Melt butter in a skillet.
> 2. Cook onion and garlic in melted butter for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
> 3. Place mixture in a large bowl to cool for 5 minutes.
> 4. Combine turkey, bread crumbs, egg, 1/4 cup of the catsup, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper with onion mixture.
> 5. Press meatloaf into an 8x4-inch loaf pan.
> 6. Spread remaining catsup on top.
> 7. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 50-55 minutes.
> 8. (Internal temperature should reach 165 degrees.) Remove from oven and let stand 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes
To ensure a moist result, use a digital meat thermometer to pull the loaf exactly at 165°F. Allowing the meatloaf to rest for the full 5 minutes after baking is vital for the structure to set, ensuring clean slices that don't crumble.
