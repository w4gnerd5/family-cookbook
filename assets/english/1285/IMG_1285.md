# Ina Garten's Unforgettable Beef Stew

A printout from Veggies By Candlelight of Ina Garten's red wine-marinated beef stew, oven-braised with carrots, potatoes, sun-dried tomatoes, and rosemary. The card has hand-strikethroughs on the red wine and the mushrooms, so the family version apparently omits both.

## Ingredients

* 2 1/2 pounds good quality beef chuck, cubed into ~1 1/2" pieces
* 1 bottle of good red wine (struck through on the card)
* 3 whole garlic cloves, smashed
* 3–4 bay leaves
* 2 cups flour (all-purpose, whole wheat pastry, or oat)
* Kosher salt
* Freshly ground black pepper
* Olive oil
* 2 yellow onions, chopped
* 1 pound carrots, peeled and cut diagonally into 1 1/2" chunks
* 1/2 pound white mushrooms, stems discarded and halved (struck through on the card)
* 1 lb small potatoes, halved or quartered
* 1 Tbsp minced garlic (~3 cloves)
* 2 cups beef stock or broth
* 1 branch fresh rosemary (or 2 small branches)
* 1/2–1 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
* 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* 1 (10 oz) package frozen peas

## Instructions

1. Marinate the beef: Place the beef in a bowl with red wine, garlic, and bay leaves. Marinate overnight in the refrigerator.
2. The next day, preheat the oven to 300°F.
3. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, 1 Tbsp salt, and pepper.
4. Lift the beef out of the marinade with a slotted spoon, discard the bay leaves and garlic, saving the marinade. In batches, dredge the beef cubes in the flour mix and shake off the excess.
5. Heat a glug of olive oil in a large pot and brown half the beef over medium heat for 5–7 minutes, turning to brown evenly. Place the beef in a large oven-proof Dutch oven and brown the remaining beef, adding oil as necessary. Place all the beef in the Dutch oven.
6. Prep the veggies: In a large pot, heat another glug of oil, then add the onions, carrots, mushrooms, and potatoes. Cook for 10 minutes over medium heat, stirring occasionally. Add the garlic and cook a couple more minutes.
7. Place all the vegetables into the Dutch oven over the beef.
8. Add 2 1/2 cups of the reserved marinade to the empty pot and cook over high heat to deglaze, scraping up the brown bits with a wooden spoon. Add the stock, rosemary, sun-dried tomatoes, Worcestershire sauce, 1/2 Tbsp salt, and 2 tsp pepper.
9. Pour the sauce over the meat and vegetables and bring to a simmer over medium heat on the stove.
10. Cover the pot and bake about 2 hours, until meat and vegetables are tender, stirring once. If the stew boils rather than simmers, lower the heat to 250 or 275°F.
11. Before serving, stir in the frozen peas, season to taste, and serve hot.

## Notes

* **Handwritten edits:** "1 bottle of good red wine" and the mushrooms line are crossed out by hand on the printout.
