# Zak Pelaccio's Crispy Pork and Watermelon Salad

This recipe highlights the balance of Southeast Asian flavors, pairing rich, fatty pork belly with the cooling crispness of watermelon. The key to the dish's texture is pressing the braised pork belly for at least 12 hours, which compresses the meat and fat into a dense, uniform layer that fries to an exceptional crunch.

For success, ensure the pork belly is refrigerated under weights for at least 12 hours to set its shape. Use a deep-fry thermometer to maintain the lard at 375°F; oil that is too cool will result in greasy pork, while oil that is too hot will burn the exterior before it becomes truly crisp.
