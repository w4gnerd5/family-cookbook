# Homemade Merguez Sausage

Merguez is a North African lamb-based sausage known for its punchy spice profile, traditionally using harissa and paprika for heat and color. The technical secret to a juicy sausage lies in temperature control; keeping the meat and fat near freezing prevents the fat from "smearing" and allows for a stable emulsion when the ice water is mixed in.

## Ingredients

> * 2 tablespoons paprika
> * 2 tablespoons Kosher salt
> * 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
> * 3 pounds lamb shoulder, cut into 3/4-inch cubes and removed of gristle
> * 1 pound lamb, beef, or pork fat, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
> * 2 tablespoons freshly minced garlic (about 6 medium cloves)
> * 1/3 cup harissa
> * 1/3 cup ice water
> * Lamb casings, soaked in warm water for 30 minutes prior to use
> 
> **Type of fire:** Direct  
> **Grill heat:** medium-high
> 
> **Special Equipment**  
> Meat grinder, Sausage stuffer, and Grill

## Notes

Keep all meat, fat, and grinder components extremely cold (near freezing) throughout the process to ensure a proper bind and prevent the fat from rendering out during cooking, which results in a dry, crumbly sausage.
