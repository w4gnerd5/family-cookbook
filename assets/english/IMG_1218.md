# Apple Cider Braised Pork Shoulder

Braising pork shoulder in apple cider utilizes a "low and slow" cooking method where the natural acidity and sugars in the cider help break down tough connective tissues over time, resulting in exceptionally tender meat and a deeply flavored, concentrated sauce.

## Notes
> * **Apple Selection:** For the best texture and flavor balance, choose firm, slightly-tart varieties like Honeycrisp, Pink Lady, or Gala that won't turn to mush during the long braising process.
> * **Ingredient Clarification:** It is vital to use apple cider (the beverage) rather than apple cider vinegar. Substituting vinegar will result in an overwhelmingly sour dish.

### Original English Text
if you notice that your pork is especially fatty, consider trimming some excess fat before searing.

* the best apples are firm, slightly-tart apples like Honeycrisp, Pink Lady, Gala, etc.

Please know that apple cider is NOT the same as apple cider vinegar--do not use vinegar in place of apple cider for this recipe.
