# Zak Pelaccio's Crispy Pork and Watermelon Salad

This recipe relies on the sharp textural and flavor contrast between rich, deep-fried braised pork and the refreshing, acidic bite of chilled watermelon and pickled rind, balanced by cooling aromatic herbs.

## Ingredients:
> ### For the Braise
> * 2 tablespoons rendered leaf lard or olive oil (the best you can afford)
> * 1 yellow onion, cut into sixths
> * 1 head of garlic, halved crosswise
> * 2 shallots, halved lengthwise
> * 2 1/2 cups chicken stock

## Notes:
* Maintain a consistent fat temperature of 375°F between frying batches to ensure the pork achieves a crisp exterior without becoming greasy.
* Serve immediately after assembly to preserve the vital temperature contrast between the warm pork and the chilled salad components.
