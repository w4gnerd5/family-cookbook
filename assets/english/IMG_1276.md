# No-Knead Bread Baking Instructions

The success of this no-knead method lies in using a preheated, heavy covered pot to trap steam released by the high-moisture dough, which creates a professional-style humid environment essential for achieving a crisp, thin crust and maximum volume.

## Ingredients:
> * Flour, wheat bran, or cornmeal (for dusting)
> * Bread dough (prepared in previous steps)

## Instructions:
> Generously coat a cotton towel (not terry cloth) with flour, wheat bran or cornmeal; put dough seam side down on towel and dust with more flour, bran or cornmeal. Cover with another cotton towel and let rise for about 2 hours. When it is ready, dough will be more than double in size and will not readily spring back when poked with a finger.
> 
> **Step 4**
> At least a half-hour before dough is ready, heat oven to 450 degrees. Put a 6- to 8-quart heavy covered pot (cast iron, enamel, Pyrex or ceramic) in oven as it heats. When dough is ready, carefully remove pot from oven. Slide your hand under towel and turn dough over into pot, seam side up; it may look like a mess, but that is OK. Shake pan once or twice if dough is unevenly distributed; it will straighten out as it bakes. Cover with lid and bake 30 minutes, then remove lid and bake another 15 to 30 minutes, until loaf is beautifully browned. Cool on a rack.

## Notes:
> Ensure the baking vessel is thoroughly preheated for at least 30 minutes to guarantee a proper crust. Avoid using terry cloth towels as the dough will stick to the loops; a smooth flour-sack towel or lint-free cotton is best.
