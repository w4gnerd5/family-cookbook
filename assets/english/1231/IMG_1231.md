# BBQ Rub

An all-purpose sweet and smoky barbecue spice rub for chicken, pork, and beef; it also works on grilled or roasted vegetables and potatoes. Printed from Dinner at the Zoo (Sara Welch); makes about 12 servings.

## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup brown sugar
* 2 tablespoons paprika
* 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
* 1 tablespoon black pepper
* 1 tablespoon kosher salt
* 1 tablespoon garlic powder
* 1 tablespoon onion powder
* 1 teaspoon mustard powder
* 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all of the ingredients together. Store in an airtight container until ready to use.
2. Spice rub keeps for up to 3 months when stored in a cool, dry place.
