# Tuscan Farmers Breakfast

This rustic breakfast dish relies on the gentle poaching of eggs directly within a savory, simmering base of beans and tomatoes. The key to its success is maintaining a consistent medium-low heat, which allows the egg whites to set firmly while the yolks remain creamy, creating a rich, self-saucing meal that is high in both protein and fiber.

## Ingredients

> * Onion
> * Fresh herbs
> * Diced tomatoes
> * Beans (type not specified, likely cannellini or borlotti)
> * Vegetable or chicken stock
> * Salt and chili flakes
> * Fresh spinach or greens (optional)
> * Eggs
> * Black pepper
> * Fresh Italian parsley
> * Gremolata (for serving)

## Instructions
> 1. Continue cooking until the mixture is golden, stirring often for about 3 more minutes; turn the heat lower if necessary.
> 2. Once the onion is tender, add the herbs and cook for one more minute.
> 3. Add the diced tomatoes, beans, and stock. Season with salt and chili flakes, then bring to a simmer on medium-low for approximately 5-7 minutes. If desired, stir in a handful of spinach or other greens at this stage.
> 4. Create small wells in the bean mixture and crack the eggs into them. Season each egg with salt and pepper. Cover the pan and simmer gently until the whites are fully set but the yolks are still soft.
> 5. To serve, sprinkle with fresh Italian parsley and spoon flavorful Gremolata over the top.

## Notes
* **Temperature Control:** Maintain a gentle simmer to ensure egg whites cook through without over-hardening the yolks.
* **Versatility:** Substitute eggs with pan-seared tofu for a vegan version, or add browned Italian sausage or Spanish chorizo for a meatier profile.
* **Serving Tip:** Using individual mini cast iron skillets allows for personalized portions and a more rustic presentation.
