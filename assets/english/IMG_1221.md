# Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

Bulgogi, literally translating to "fire meat," is a cornerstone of Korean cuisine typically made with thinly sliced marinated beef. This skillet version adapts the traditional sweet and savory flavor profile—driven by soy sauce, brown sugar, and toasted sesame oil—to chicken, allowing for a rapid caramelization that creates a rich glaze in a fraction of the time.

## Ingredients
> * 1/4 cup chopped onion
> * 5 tablespoons soy sauce
> * 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
> * 2 tablespoons minced garlic
> * 2 tablespoons sesame oil
> * 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
> * 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
> * Salt and ground black pepper to taste
> * 1 pound skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into thin strips

## Instructions
> ### Step 1
> Whisk onion, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds, cayenne pepper, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl until marinade is smooth.
>
> ### Step 2
> Cook and stir chicken and marinade together in a large skillet over medium-high heat until chicken is cooked through, about 15 minutes.

## Notes
> * For the best results, slice the chicken into uniform, very thin strips to ensure even cooking and to maximize the surface area for the sauce to thicken and glaze.
> * Maintain a steady medium-high heat; this allows the sugars to caramelize and "stick" to the meat without burning the soy sauce.
