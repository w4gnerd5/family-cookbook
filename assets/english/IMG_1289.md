# Braised Short Ribs

Braised short ribs rely on a low-and-slow cooking process to transform tough connective tissue into succulent gelatin, resulting in a rich, flavorful dish where the sauce is concentrated after the vegetables have yielded their essence to the braising liquid.

## Ingredients
> *Note: A complete ingredient list is not visible on this page fragment; however, the text mentions short ribs, aromatic vegetables, parsley, chives, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.*

## Instructions
> ### Step 5
> Using tongs, remove the ribs from the pot, taking care (for presentation purposes, really) not to let the bone slip out and transfer them to a large plate. (While you could serve the short ribs right out of this pot, the vegetables have all given up their flavor and texture and aren't worth much now, so feel free to strain the sauce for easier eating.) Scatter parsley, chives and lemon zest over the top of the short ribs. Separate the fat from the sauce, season with salt and pepper and serve alongside.

## Notes
> Straining the sauce before serving is essential for a refined texture, as the braising vegetables will have become overly soft and lost their structural appeal after long cooking.
