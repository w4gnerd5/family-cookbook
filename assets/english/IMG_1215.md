# Holiday Chicken Salad

This classic holiday-style chicken salad utilizes a combination of sweet dried cranberries and crunchy pecans to create a festive balance of textures and flavors. The addition of bell pepper and green onion provides a savory depth that offsets the creamy mayonnaise base, making it a versatile dish suitable for both party platters and sandwich fillings.

## Ingredients:
> - 4 cups cubed, cooked chicken meat
> - 1 cup mayonnaise
> - 1 teaspoon paprika
> - 1 1/2 cups dried cranberries
> - 1 cup chopped celery
> - 2 green onions, chopped
> - 1/2 cup minced green bell pepper
> - 1 cup chopped pecans
> - 1 teaspoon seasoning salt
> - ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions:
> 1. In a medium bowl, mix together mayonnaise with paprika and seasoned salt. Blend in dried cranberries, celery, bell pepper, onion, and nuts. Add chopped chicken, and mix well. Season with black pepper to taste. Chill 1 hour.

## Notes:
- **Temperature Control:** Ensure the cooked chicken is fully chilled before mixing to maintain the best texture and food safety.
- **Flavor Development:** Do not skip the one-hour chilling period; this allows the dried cranberries to hydrate slightly and the flavors of the seasoning salt and onions to meld with the dressing.
