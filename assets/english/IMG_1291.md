# Tuscan Farmers Breakfast

This rustic dish is a variation of traditional Italian country breakfasts, where protein-rich beans and eggs provide long-lasting satiety for physical labor. The combination of slow-cooked aromatics and acidic tomatoes creates a flavorful poaching liquid that tenderizes the beans while infusing the eggs with deep savory notes.

## Ingredients
> * 1–2 tablespoons olive oil
> * 1/2 an onion, diced
> * 4 garlic cloves, rough chopped
> * 1 tablespoon chopped rosemary, sage or thyme (or sub 1 1/2 teaspoons dried Italian seasoning)
> * 1 14 ounce can diced tomatoes (fire roasted are nice) or sub 2 medium tomatoes, diced with their juices
> * 1 14.5 ounce can cannellini beans or white beans (or 1 1/2–2 cups cooked), rinsed and drained
> * 1 cup stock or broth (or water plus 1 bouillon cube) either chicken or veggie
> * 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
> * 1/4 teaspoon chili flakes, more to taste
> * 4–6 eggs (for vegans serve with pan-seared tofu)
> * Serve with crusty bread or warm tortillas and Gremolata
> * Optional addition: spinach

## Instructions
> In a 10–11 inch skillet, heat the oil and saute the onions for 3 minutes over medium-high heat. Turn heat to medium and add the garlic. Saute until fragrant.

## Notes
To ensure the best texture, use a heavy-bottomed skillet to maintain steady heat, which allows the eggs to poach gently in the sauce without scorching the beans at the bottom.
