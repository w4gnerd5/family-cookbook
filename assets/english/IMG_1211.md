# Cashew Weight to Volume Conversion

Converting raw cashews by weight to a ground volume is crucial for consistent results in recipes requiring nut flours, as grinding changes the density and surface area, significantly altering how the ingredient occupies space in standard measuring cups.

## Ingredients:
> * 2 pounds (#) raw cashews

## Instructions:
> * 2# raw cashews = 6 cups ground

## Notes:
> When measuring ground cashews, spoon the meal into the cup and level it off without packing it down to ensure the measurement remains accurate to this conversion.

### Original English Text:
2# raw cashews = 6 cups ground
