# Indian Pudding

A classic New England baked cornmeal and molasses pudding, slow-baked for hours and served chilled with lightly whipped cream. The cold milk poured over the top before baking keeps the center soft.

## Ingredients

* 1 quart plus 1/2 cup (1 L plus 120 ml) milk, divided
* 1/2 cup (80 g) cornmeal
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 cup (180 g) molasses
* 1 1/2 teaspoons ground ginger or cinnamon
* Lightly whipped cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 300°F / 150°C. Butter the bottom and sides of a 9 x 9 inch baking dish.
2. Place 1 quart (1 L) milk in a saucepan or double boiler. Heat to about 180°F / 82°C, until small bubbles form at the edges, stirring frequently so it does not burn or form a skin.
3. In a bowl, whisk together the 1/2 cup cornmeal and 1 teaspoon salt.
4. When the milk reaches temperature, reduce heat to low. Slowly whisk in the cornmeal–salt mixture and cook for 10 minutes, stirring constantly.
5. Stir in 1/2 cup molasses; cook and stir for 2 minutes.
6. Stir in 1 1/2 teaspoons ginger or cinnamon (sift ground ginger to avoid clumps). Cook another 1 minute, until smooth.
7. Pour the hot batter into the prepared baking dish. Gently pour the remaining 1/2 cup (120 ml) cold milk over the top. Do not stir it in.
8. Bake for 3 to 4 hours. A darker top and a thin film/skin are normal.
9. Let the pudding cool completely so it firms up, up to 2 hours. Cover and refrigerate overnight or until completely chilled and set. Scoop into bowls, top with lightly whipped cream, and serve.

## Notes

* **Skin:** Parchment paper pressed onto the surface while chilling prevents a thick skin; leave uncovered for the traditional "skin."
