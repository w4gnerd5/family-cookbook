# Huevos Rancheros Recipe (Page 4)

Huevos rancheros is a classic Mexican breakfast dish that relies on the textural contrast between a crisp corn tortilla base and rich, runny egg yolks. The dish's success depends on the balance of acidity from the salsa and the creaminess of the eggs and beans, creating a deeply satisfying and savory morning meal.
