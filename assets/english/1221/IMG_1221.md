# Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

A quick Korean-style bulgogi made with chicken breast strips in a soy, brown sugar, garlic, and sesame marinade, cooked entirely in one skillet in 30 minutes. Printed from Allrecipes; serve over rice.

## Ingredients

* 1/4 cup chopped onion
* 5 tablespoons soy sauce
* 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
* 2 tablespoons minced garlic
* 2 tablespoons sesame oil
* 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
* Salt and ground black pepper to taste
* 1 pound skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into thin strips

## Instructions

1. Whisk onion, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds, cayenne pepper, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl until marinade is smooth.
2. Cook and stir chicken and marinade together in a large skillet over medium-high heat until chicken is cooked through, about 15 minutes.

## Notes

* **Variations:** the author adds carrot straws and sliced mushrooms, and suggests beef or pork instead of chicken; peanut oil can replace the sesame oil.
