# Baked Salmon with Dill Garlic Beurre Blanc

A beurre blanc is a classic French emulsified butter sauce; here, the acidity of the reduced white wine provides the structural backbone to suspend the milk solids of the cold butter, creating a rich, velvety emulsion that perfectly complements the fatty profile of oven-baked salmon.

## Ingredients
> * 2 lb. salmon, fresh, fillets
> * 1/2 C. white wine
> * 8 Tbs. butter, cold, cut into 16 pieces
> * 1 tsp. dill weed
> * 1/2 tsp. garlic, minced

## Instructions
> Preheat the oven to 375 degrees. Bake the salmon for 12-15 minutes. The fish should be firm but not dry. Place the wine, dill and garlic in a small saucepan. Reduce on high heat until almost dry. Remove from the heat and quickly add all the butter while whisking briskly. When all the butter has been incorporated, allow to rest 10 minutes. Spoon over the hot salmon.

## Notes
To ensure a successful emulsion, make sure the butter is refrigerator-cold and the pan is removed from the direct heat source before whisking it in; if the mixture becomes too hot, the sauce will "break" and turn into clarified oil.
