# Roast Pork Belly Recipe Notes

Pork belly relies on a slow-cooking process to tenderize the meat and render fat, followed by high-heat exposure to transform the skin into a perfectly crisp crackling, with fennel seeds providing a classic aromatic profile that balances the dish's richness.

## Ingredients
> *   Fennel powder (or 1 tsp lightly toasted fennel seeds ground to yield 1/2 tsp)
> *   Cauliflower Mash (pureed thoroughly for Cauliflower Puree)

## Instructions
> *   **Preparation of Fennel Powder:** If grinding your own, toast 1 tsp of fennel seeds lightly, then use a mortar and pestle or spice grinder to produce 1/2 tsp of powder.
> *   **Cauliflower Puree:** Follow the standard Cauliflower Mash recipe but puree more thoroughly to achieve a smooth consistency.
> *   **Storage:** Store leftovers for up to 3 - 5 days.
> *   **Reheating:** Separate the crackling from the meat. Reheat the meat in a microwave. Reheat the crackling in a hot oven to maintain its crispy texture. Alternatively, slice and pan-fry the meat or serve it cold in salads and sandwiches.

## Notes
For the best texture, always reheat crackling in the oven rather than the microwave to prevent it from becoming rubbery. Be aware that the provided nutritional data includes all fat, including rendered fat that may be discarded, meaning actual calorie intake is likely lower.
