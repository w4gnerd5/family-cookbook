# Homemade Merguez Sausage Recipe

Merguez is a signature North African lamb sausage known for its intense heat and earthy undertones, typically derived from a blend of harissa and toasted spices. The culinary success of this recipe relies on the release of essential oils from whole seeds during the toasting process, which creates a complex aromatic profile that balances the rich, gamey flavor of the lamb.

## Notes
To ensure the best texture, keep the meat and all grinding equipment thoroughly chilled (near freezing) to prevent the fat from melting and breaking the emulsion. Toast the whole seeds only until they become fragrant to avoid developing any burnt or bitter notes.
