# Homemade Granola

Homemade granola relies on the combination of a liquid fat and a natural sweetener to evenly coat dry oats and nuts. During the slow-baking process, moisture evaporates while the sugars caramelize, creating the classic crispy clusters and deep golden-brown flavor profile characteristic of high-quality breakfast cereals.

## Ingredients

> *   4 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
> *   2 cups sweetened shredded coconut
> *   2 cups sliced almonds
> *   3/4 cup vegetable oil
> *   1/2 cup good honey
> *   1 1/2 cups small diced dried apricots
> *   1 cup small diced dried figs
> *   1 cup dried cherries
> *   1 cup dried cranberries
> *   1 cup roasted, unsalted cashews

## Instructions

> Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
> 
> Toss the oats, coconut, and almonds together in a large bowl. Whisk together the oil and honey in a small bowl. Pour the liquids over the oat mixture and stir with a wooden spoon until all the oats and nuts are coated. Pour onto a 13 by 18 by 1-inch sheet pan. Bake, stirring occasionally with a spatula, until the mixture turns a nice, even, golden brown, about 45 minutes.
> 
> Remove the granola from the oven and allow to cool, stirring occasionally. Add the apricots, figs, cherries, cranberries, and cashews. Store the cooled granola in an airtight container.

## Notes

*   **Monitor the Oven:** Watch the granola closely during the final 10 minutes of baking, as the coconut and honey can transition from golden to burnt very quickly.
*   **Cool Completely:** Ensure the mixture is entirely cool before adding the dried fruit and sealing it in a container; residual heat will create steam, resulting in a soggy texture rather than a crunchy one.
