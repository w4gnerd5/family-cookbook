# Chicken, Rice, and Spices Bake

This one-pan meal utilizes the absorption method of cooking, where the rice is steamed directly in the oven using chicken stock and the natural juices released from the meat. Covering the dish tightly with foil is the scientific key to this recipe, as it traps steam to ensure the grains hydrate fully and evenly without drying out.

## Notes
A tight seal with the aluminum foil is essential; if steam escapes, the rice will remain crunchy and undercooked. For the best results, let the dish rest for 5 minutes after removing it from the oven to allow the moisture to redistribute through the rice.
