# Entrees: Mid-Century Vegetarian Specialties

These recipes reflect mid-century American vegetarianism, particularly the traditions that utilized nut meats and legumes to create hearty "meatless" loaves and burgers. The use of binders like breadcrumbs and eggs, combined with savory seasonings like sage and yeast extracts (e.g., Savita), demonstrates the culinary science of creating umami-rich textures and flavors without animal proteins.

## Instructions

> **Basic White Sauce**
> Melt shortening, stir in flour and cook 1 minute over low heat. Add milk gradually, stirring until smooth and thick. 
> *   *Thin White Sauce:* Use 1 tablespoon each flour and shortening.
> *   *Thick White Sauce:* Use 3 tablespoons each flour and shortening.
> 
> **Asparagus Loaf**
> Beat eggs, add seasoning, hot milk, cracker crumbs. Carefully fold in diced asparagus. Bake in oiled casserole at 350° about 40 minutes. Serve with parsley white sauce. Serves 8.
> 
> **Baked Beans**
> Cook beans in plenty of water until nearly done. Bake in bean pot with all other ingredients 2-3 hours at 250°. Serves 6. 
> *Alternative Method:* Use 1/2 cup molasses and 1 tablespoonful brown sugar. Bury a whole peeled onion in the bean crock, and bake for 6 hours, keeping the beans covered with water.
> 
> **Bean-Burgers**
> Chop the baked beans in wooden chopping bowl. Add remaining ingredients with catchup (or chili sauce or leftover stewed tomatoes) sufficient to moisten. Shape into small chops. Dust with extra bread crumbs. Sauté to a delicate brown. Stick a wooden skewer into narrow end for a bone. Serve with chili sauce or catchup.
> 
> **Cashew Nut Loaf**
> Put nuts, gluten and onions through food chopper, coarse blade. Combine all ingredients and mix well. Pour in oiled and crumbed brick-shape tins. Bake covered in pan of water for 80 minutes (originally 60) at 350°. Serve hot or cold with mushroom or cranberry sauce. Any raw or slightly toasted nuts may be used in place of the cashews. Serves 10-12.

## Notes

*   **Texture Control:** For the Bean-Burgers, ensure the mixture is moist enough to hold its shape but dry enough to sauté; extra breadcrumbs help achieve a crisp exterior.
*   **Baking the Nut Loaf:** Use a water bath (bain-marie) to prevent the loaf from drying out or burning. The handwritten notes suggest extending the bake time to 80 minutes, then uncovering and baking for an additional 20 minutes (or until brown) after removing it from the water bath.
*   **Flavor Substitutes:** If Savita is unavailable, soy sauce can be used as a substitute for a similar savory profile.
