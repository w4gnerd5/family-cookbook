# Chicken, Rice, and Spices Bake

The success of this oven-baked dish relies on the "pilaf" technique and steam retention. By toasting the rice in oil and spices before adding liquid, you coat the starch, preventing the grains from sticking together. Sealing the dish tightly with foil creates a pressurized environment that cooks the long-grain rice evenly with the chicken, ensuring the grains remain separate and fluffy while fully absorbing the aromatic, fat-soluble spices like turmeric and cumin.

## Ingredients

> * 4 cups chicken stock
> * 2 tablespoons olive oil
> * 8 -10 boneless skinless chicken thighs
> * 1 medium onion
> * 1 garlic clove (chopped, minced or crushed)
> * 1/2-3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
> * 1 1/2 teaspoons ground turmeric
> * 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
> * 1 1/2 teaspoons curry powder
> * 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional) or 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (optional)
> * 3/4-1 teaspoon salt
> * 2 cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained or 2 cups regular medium grain rice or 2 cups long-grain rice

## Instructions

> 1. Preheat oven to 375°F and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
> 2. Bring water to boiling and add chicken bouillon cubes, lower heat and simmer till dissolved--keep it hot.
> 3. Sprinkle chicken with half the spices (except the garlic).
> 4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet on med-high to high heat and fry the chicken till both sides are brown, lower heat to medium and remove the chicken to a plate.
> 5. In that same skillet sauté the onions for 2 minutes, then add the garlic and remaining spices and sauté another 2 minutes, then turn off the burner.
> 6. Add the rice and stir for about 30 seconds to 1 minute, till well coated.

## Notes

* **Cinnamon Adjustment:** Ensure you only use 1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon of cinnamon; the original recipe mistakenly suggested 2 teaspoons, which is overpowering.
* **Salt Control:** The salt has been reduced to 3/4-1 teaspoon to prevent the dish from becoming overly salty, as the chicken stock/bouillon already contains significant sodium.
* **Perfect Rice:** For the best results with basmati rice, ensure the baking dish is covered very tightly with foil before putting it in the oven to trap the steam effectively.
