# Bacon and Gruyere Breakfast Casserole

A blended egg, Gruyère, and cream cheese custard baked gently in a water bath and topped with crumbled bacon. From Cuts and Crumbles; the water bath keeps the texture custardy rather than rubbery.

## Ingredients

* 8 slices of bacon
* 9 eggs
* 3/4 cup shredded Gruyère cheese
* 1/3 cup cream cheese
* 1/2 tsp salt
* Fresh chives or parsley, to garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook and crumble the bacon. Use the excess bacon fat to coat a baking dish (an 8x8 glass dish is ideal).
2. Move your oven racks so one rack is right in the middle, then preheat your oven to 350 degrees.
3. Shred the Gruyère and place the cheese, eggs, softened cream cheese, and salt into a blender. Blend on medium-high speed until smooth, about 1 minute. Pour into the greased baking dish and top with most of the crumbled bacon, reserving some for the end.
4. Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil. Set a roasting pan (or other large pan) onto the oven rack and fill with 6 cups of very hot tap water. Place the dish of eggs inside the water pan.
5. Bake for 55 minutes to 1 hour, until the eggs are just set.
6. Remove the water pan and dish from the oven and remove foil. To brown the top, switch the oven to broil and return the uncovered dish for about 4–5 minutes, until slightly browned.
7. Top with a few more crumbles of bacon and diced fresh chives or parsley, if desired.
8. Cut into 9 slices and serve.

## Notes

* **Leftovers:** Keep refrigerated airtight up to 1 week; microwave each piece about 45 seconds.
* **Timing:** Prep 5 mins, cook 1 hour; yield 9 servings.
