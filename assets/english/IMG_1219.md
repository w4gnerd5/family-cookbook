# Lucky and Rippy's Favorite Dog Food

This homemade diet leverages the high digestibility of lean chicken and white rice, a combination frequently recommended by veterinarians for dogs with sensitive stomachs or as a soft food alternative. By utilizing a finely minced vegetable blend devoid of toxic alliums like onions and garlic, it provides essential fiber and nutrients in a texture that integrates easily with standard kibble.

## Ingredients
> * 3 pounds minced chicken meat
> * 4 cups rice
> * 2 ½ cups frozen vegetable blend (no garlic or onions), minced
> * 6 ½ cups water

## Instructions
> **Step 1**
> Gather all ingredients.
>
> **Step 2**
> Place chicken, rice, and vegetables into a large saucepan or Dutch oven; stir in water until smooth. Place over medium-high heat and bring to a boil; stir constantly.
>
> **Step 3**
> Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed, about 25 minutes. Cool completely before serving; store covered in the refrigerator.

## Notes
Always double-check that your vegetable mix does not contain onions, garlic, chives, or leeks, as these are toxic to canines. For safety, allow the food to cool to room temperature before serving or refrigerating to prevent thermal burns to your pet and to maintain food safety standards.
