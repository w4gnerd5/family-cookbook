# Tuscan Farmers Breakfast

Sylvia Fountaine's Feasting At Home skillet of cannellini beans simmered with tomatoes and herbs, with eggs cracked into wells and gently steamed. Served with crusty bread or tortillas and gremolata.

## Ingredients

* 1–2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1/2 an onion, diced
* 4 garlic cloves, rough chopped
* 1 tablespoon chopped rosemary, sage or thyme (or sub 1 1/2 teaspoons dried Italian seasoning)
* 1 14 ounce can diced tomatoes (fire roasted are nice) or sub 2 medium tomatoes, diced with their juices
* 1 14.5 ounce can cannellini beans or white beans (or 1 1/2–2 cups cooked), rinsed and drained
* 1 cup stock or broth (or water plus 1 bouillon cube), either chicken or veggie
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1/4 teaspoon chili flakes, more to taste
* 4–6 eggs (for vegans serve with pan-seared tofu)
* Crusty bread or warm tortillas and Gremolata, for serving
* Optional addition: spinach

## Instructions

1. In a 10–11 inch skillet, heat the oil and saute the onions for 3 minutes over medium-high heat. Turn heat to medium and add the garlic. Saute until fragrant and golden, stirring often, about 3 more minutes, turning heat lower if necessary. Once the onion is tender, add the herbs and cook one more minute.
2. Add diced tomatoes, beans and stock. Add salt and chili flakes and bring to a simmer on medium-low about 5–7 minutes. At this point, stir in a handful of spinach or greens if you like.
3. Make little wells in the beans and crack the eggs into them. Season each egg with salt and pepper, cover and simmer gently until whites are set but yolks are still soft.
4. Sprinkle with fresh Italian parsley and spoon gremolata over top.

## Notes

* **Variations:** For a Spanish take, add 1/4 cup thinly sliced cured Spanish chorizo and 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, or brown Italian sausage with the onions. Vegan: pan-seared tofu instead of eggs.
* **Timing:** Prep 10, cook 20, total 30 minutes; yield 4.
