# Zak Pelaccio's Crispy Pork and Watermelon Salad

This dish balances fatty, rich braised pork belly with refreshing watermelon and a punchy Thai-inspired dressing. The key to the pork's texture is a low-temperature braise followed by a pressing step, which compresses the meat and fat to ensure a perfectly uniform sear and a melt-in-your-mouth interior.

## Notes

*   **Temperature Control:** Maintain a low oven temperature (225°F) for the braise to ensure the pork stays succulent without the fat rendering out too quickly.
*   **The Press:** Do not skip step 10; pressing the cooked belly between flat pans while it cools is essential for creating the dense, uniform texture needed for perfect crispy cubes later.
