### 3. The Transcribed Text
If you'd rather recreate the note with a digital font to make it perfectly crisp for your project, here is the text:

> **Pelmennye Testo na Kipyatke (Super)**
> **Dumpling Dough with Boiling Water (Super)**
> *   1 egg
> *   Salt
> *   3 cups flour
> *   1 tbsp vegetable oil
> *   1 cup boiling water
> 
> **Instructions:**
> Add salt to the egg and beat with a fork. Then add 3 cups of flour and a spoon of vegetable oil, mix well, and add 1 cup of boiling water. Mix with a spoon, and then knead with your hands (if necessary, add a little more flour).
