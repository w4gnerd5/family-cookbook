
### **Transcription & Translation**
**Title (Russian):** 
*   **Людмила** (Lyudmila)
*   **Бивстроген** (Beef Stroganoff - *phonetic spelling*)

**Recipe (Georgian):**
*   Cut the meat (finely) into a pan.
*   Simmer/stew without oil.
*   Add onions and sauté; add salt and pepper.
*   Add finely chopped bell pepper and crushed tomatoes (fresh + canned).
*   Add a little tomato juice.
*   Finally, mix 1 tbsp flour and starch dissolved in cold water (use 1 tsp starch in 1/2 cup water).
*   Pour in and bring to a boil.
