# Changelog

One entry per edition of the book. The version is set in `book.yaml` and
printed in the colophon; `scripts/release.sh` publishes each one as a GitHub
Release. Changes to a single recipe live in git:
`git log --follow assets/<group>/<n>/<file>.md`.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-05)

First numbered edition, cut before the alpha dummy so the download link exists.

- 75 recipes, 201 pages, A4 with 3mm bleed for a ring binder
- Seven sections: Breads & Doughs (20), Soups & Stews (5), Mains (29),
  Sides & Salads (4), Preserves & Sauces (4), Desserts & Sweets (9), Pantry (4)
- 39 recipes from the family's Georgian and Russian papers, 36 in English
- Recipe numbers are frozen from this edition on

Still open before alpha: three low-resolution AI dish renders, the art
direction pass over the versos, the book's real title, and the QR code (it
waits on the domain).
